Think clearly

How the mind decides, and how to focus it

  1. Thinking, Fast and SlowDaniel Kahneman · 18 minYour mind runs on a fast, automatic storyteller that feels sure and a slow, lazy checker that mostly signs off, so thinking better means learning the storyteller's tricks by name and slowing down when the stakes are high.
  2. Deep WorkCal Newport · 20 minThe power to focus without distraction on hard work is getting rarer and worth more every year, so people who build their work life around it will thrive.
  3. The 80/20 PrincipleRichard Koch · 17 minA few causes make most of the results. Find your few, give them more, and give the rest less, at work and in life.
  4. Dopamine DetoxThibaut Meurisse · 15 minConstant stimulation makes your important work feel dull. Lower the noise, and hard things get easier to start.
  5. InfluenceRobert B. Cialdini · 19 minMost of our yes decisions come from a few mental shortcuts. Each shortcut usually points the right way, and each can be used on us by someone who wants a yes.
  6. Talking to StrangersMalcolm Gladwell · 19 minWe misread strangers because we start by believing them, trust faces and manner that do not show feelings, and ignore the setting, so we should meet them with caution and humility.
