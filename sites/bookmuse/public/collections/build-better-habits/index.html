Build better habits

Start small, keep going

  1. Atomic HabitsJames Clear · 17 minSmall habits, repeated every day, build the person you want to become. Make good habits obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying, and flip those four laws to break bad ones.
  2. Million Dollar HabitsStellan Moreira · 16 minYour life follows your habits, so build the habits of a calm, happy mind first, then the habits that grow your money.
  3. The 5 AM ClubRobin Sharma · 14 minGet up at 5 AM and spend the first hour on yourself in three parts, move, reflect and grow, and keep it up for 66 days until it is automatic, while you guard your focus and your rest.
  4. The Science of Self-DisciplinePeter Hollins · 18 minSelf-discipline is a skill you can train, and it works best when you also shape your surroundings, your people and your habits so that you need less willpower.
  5. No ExcusesBrian Tracy · 17 minSelf-discipline, doing the right thing at the right time even when you do not feel like it, is the one skill that makes every other success rule work, and anyone can build it as a habit.
  6. The ONE ThingGary Keller, Jay Papasan · 16 minBig results come from going small: find the ONE Thing that makes everything else easier or unnecessary, and do it first.
  7. The 7 Habits of Highly Effective PeopleStephen R. Covey · 19 minLasting change works from the inside out: change how you see things, then build seven habits that take you from depending on others, to standing on your own, to working well with others.
