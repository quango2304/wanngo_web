Gentler ways to live with an anxious mind

  1. How to Stop Worrying And Start LivingDale Carnegie · 17 minWorry wears down your body and clouds your mind, but you can break the habit by living one day at a time, facing the worst with clear facts, and choosing calm action.
  2. The Power of NowEckhart Tolle · 19 minYou are not your mind. The endless thinking in your head, and the old pain it feeds on, cause most of your suffering, and you end it by watching them and giving this moment your full attention.
  3. My Age of AnxietyScott Stossel · 18 minAnxiety is fear of something that has not happened yet. It grows from body, mind, genes, childhood and stress, so no single cure covers it.
  4. 13 Things Mentally Strong People Don't DoAmy Morin · 19 minYou are only as strong as your worst habits, so mental strength means dropping thirteen habits of thinking, feeling and acting that hold you back.
  5. Don’t Overthink ItAnne Bogel · 17 minOverthinking eats the hours of your life. You can win them back with small shifts: aim for good enough, decide once, and say yes to what you love.
  6. The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ckMark Manson · 17 minA good life does not come from caring about more and feeling good all the time. It comes from choosing the few things worth caring about, and accepting the pain that comes with them.
