People skills

Talk, listen and read the room

  1. How to Win Friends and Influence People in the Digital AgeDale Carnegie & Associates · 20 minCarnegie's old rules still work online: you win friends and influence people by leaving each one a little better, and that starts with real interest in them.
  2. How to Talk to AnyoneLeil Lowndes · 16 minSuccess with people comes from small moves you can learn, and every one of them aims at the same thing: making the other person feel special and at ease.
  3. Read People Like a BookPatrick King · 19 minYou cannot see inside another person, so reading people means making careful guesses from many small clues, never trusting one sure sign.
  4. Emotional IntelligenceDaniel Goleman · 19 minFeelings can swamp your thinking, or they can guide it. Goleman says five skills decide which one happens, and that these skills can matter as much as IQ, and sometimes more.
  5. The 48 Laws of PowerRobert Greene · 19 minPower is a game that everyone plays, and Greene says you do better when you see how it works: keep a calm face, hide your aims, manage your image, learn what people want, stop in time, and stay hard to pin down.
  6. Drama FreeNedra Glover Tawwab · 18 minYou cannot change your family, but you can change how you take part in it, and that is enough to keep a hard relationship on your terms or to end it.
