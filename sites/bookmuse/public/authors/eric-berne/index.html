Eric Berne

EB

Canadian-born psychiatrist who created transactional analysis · 1910–1970

Eric Berne was a psychiatrist born in Montreal, Canada. He trained at McGill University and later worked in the United States, where he studied psychiatry at Yale and psychoanalysis in San Francisco. He believed people can be understood by studying what happens between them, and not only what happens inside one mind. From this he built transactional analysis, which describes three states of mind and the repeating patterns he called games. His book Games People Play (1964) made these ideas famous and sold millions of copies. He died in California in 1970.
