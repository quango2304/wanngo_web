Elaine Mazlish

EM

American parenting educator and writer · 1925–2017

Elaine Mazlish was an American writer and parent educator. She studied with the child psychologist Dr. Haim Ginott and taught his methods in workshops for parents and teachers. With Adele Faber she wrote How to Talk So Kids Will Listen & Listen So Kids Will Talk in 1980 and Siblings Without Rivalry. She died in 2017.
