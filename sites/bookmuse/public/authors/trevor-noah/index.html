Trevor Noah

South African comedian, writer and television host · Born 1984

Trevor Noah was born in Johannesburg, South Africa, in 1984. He became a comedian and writer in South Africa, and in 2015 he took over as host of the American television show The Daily Show, which he left in 2022. His memoir Born a Crime, about growing up in South Africa as the child of a black mother and a white father, came out in 2016. He has also written Into the Uncut Grass (2024) and has won two Primetime Emmy Awards.
