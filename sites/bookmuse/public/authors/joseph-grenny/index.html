Joseph Grenny

Author, speaker and cofounder of Crucial Learning

Joseph Grenny is a cofounder of Crucial Learning, the company once called VitalSmarts, which he started in 1990. He earned a BA at Brigham Young University and earlier worked at the Covey Leadership Center, where he met Ron McMillan. He and his partners built the book from a course they taught at Lockheed Martin in 1997. He also co-wrote Crucial Accountability, Change Anything and Influencer, and his books have sold over five million copies.
