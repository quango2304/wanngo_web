Richard Koch

RK

British management consultant, investor and author · Born 1950

Richard Koch is a British author, management consultant and investor. He studied at Oxford and the Wharton School, then worked as a partner at Bain & Company. He later helped found the consultancy L.E.K. and backed businesses such as Filofax, Plymouth Gin and Betfair. His book The 80/20 Principle, first published in 1997, applies an old idea about wealth to business and to personal life.
