Brianna Wiest

BW

American writer on personal growth

Brianna Wiest is an American writer of personal-growth essays and books. She began as a journalist and first shared her work on the website Thought Catalog, where she later became a partner; she has also written for Forbes, Teen Vogue and HuffPost. Her first big book was 101 Essays That Will Change The Way You Think, from 2016. Her later book The Mountain Is You (2020), about self-sabotage, became an international bestseller.
