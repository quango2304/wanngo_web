Robert I. Sutton

American professor of management science at Stanford · Born 1954

Robert I. Sutton is an American professor of management science and engineering at Stanford University, where he has taught since 1983. He studies how organizations work and promotes evidence-based management, which means using facts to make decisions. He is a fellow at the design firm IDEO and helped found Stanford's d.school. His books include The No Asshole Rule, Good Boss, Bad Boss and Weird Ideas That Work. He wrote Scaling Up Excellence with Huggy Rao in 2014.
