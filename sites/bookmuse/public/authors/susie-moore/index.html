Susie Moore

SM

English-born life coach, advice columnist and author

Susie Moore is a life coach, advice columnist and author who was born in England. She lived in Australia and New York, and worked as a sales director for start-ups before she became a full-time coach and writer. She says she has no formal coaching qualifications. Her books include What If It Does Work Out?, Stop Checking Your Likes (2020) and Let It Be Easy. She now lives in Miami.
