Eric Klinenberg

EK

American sociologist and professor at New York University

Eric Klinenberg is an American sociologist who teaches at New York University. He studies cities and how people live together. His books include Heat Wave, about a deadly summer in Chicago, and Going Solo, about the rise of living alone. He worked with the comedian Aziz Ansari on Modern Romance.
