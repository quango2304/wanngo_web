Alex Hormozi

AH

Entrepreneur and business author

Alex Hormozi is an entrepreneur and author. He owned a chain of gyms, and later started Gym Launch, a company that helps gym owners get more members. He now runs Acquisition.com, a group of companies he invests in. He wrote $100M Offers in 2021 as the first book in a series about growing a business.
