Stephen King

SK

American author of horror and suspense novels · Born 1947

Stephen King was born in Portland, Maine, in 1947. His first published novel, Carrie, came out in 1974. He went on to write many horror, fantasy and suspense books, among them The Shining, The Stand, Misery and The Dark Tower series. He received the National Book Foundation's medal for Distinguished Contribution to American Letters in 2003 and a National Medal of Arts. He is married to the novelist Tabitha King.
