Ray Dalio

American investor and founder of Bridgewater Associates · Born 1949

Ray Dalio grew up in a middle-class family on Long Island, New York. He began investing as a boy, with money he earned caddying. In 1975, at age 26, he started the investment firm Bridgewater Associates in his two-bedroom apartment. He ran it for more than forty years and grew it into a large and well-known firm. In Principles (2017) he shares the rules for life and work that he says he learned from his mistakes.

www.principles.com
