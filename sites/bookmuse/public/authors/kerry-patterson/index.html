Kerry Patterson

Author, teacher and cofounder of Crucial Learning

Kerry Patterson taught at Brigham Young University's business school and spent ten years as a research and development leader at Interact Performance Systems. He did doctoral work at Stanford and won BYU's Mentor of the Year award. In 1990 he cofounded VitalSmarts, now Crucial Learning, with the other authors of this book. He co-wrote four New York Times bestsellers, including Crucial Confrontations, Influencer and Change Anything. He died in 2022.
