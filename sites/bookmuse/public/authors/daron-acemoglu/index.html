Daron Acemoglu

Economist at MIT and co-author of Why Nations Fail · Born 1967

Daron Acemoglu is an economist born in Istanbul, Turkey. He studied at the University of York and earned his PhD at the London School of Economics in 1992. He has taught at MIT since 1993 and became an Institute Professor there in 2019. He won the John Bates Clark Medal in 2005. In 2012 he and James A. Robinson published Why Nations Fail, and in 2024 they shared the Nobel Prize in economics with Simon Johnson.

Summaries of Daron Acemoglu's books

  1. Why Nations FailDaron Acemoglu and James A. Robinson · 22 minNations are rich or poor because of their institutions, the rules that decide who can work, invest and hold power. Rules that share power make a country rich. Rules that let a few take from the many keep it poor.
