Mrs. Hinch

MH

British cleaning influencer and author · Born 1990

Sophie Hinchliffe, known as Mrs Hinch, is an English influencer from Essex. She became well known for sharing home-cleaning tips on Instagram, where she calls cleaning "hinching". She has said she lives with anxiety and that cleaning helps calm her. Her first book, Hinch Yourself Happy, came out in 2019. She has since written other books, including a memoir and a children's book.
