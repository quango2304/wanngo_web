Marc Schulz

American psychologist and associate director of the Harvard Study of Adult Development

Marc Schulz is a clinical psychologist and a professor of psychology at Bryn Mawr College, where he also directs the data science program. He is the associate director of the Harvard Study of Adult Development. His research looks at close relationships and how they shape health and well-being through adult life. He studied at Amherst College and the University of California at Berkeley, and he is a practicing therapist. The Good Life (2023) is his book with Robert Waldinger.
