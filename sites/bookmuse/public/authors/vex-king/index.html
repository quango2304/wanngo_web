Vex King

British writer and mind coach

Vex King is a British writer and mind coach. His father died when he was a baby, and his family was often homeless while he grew up in rough neighborhoods. He began sharing quotes and advice on Instagram, where many people found him. His first book, Good Vibes, Good Life, became a number one Sunday Times bestseller, and he followed it with Healing Is the New High.
