Napoleon Hill

American writer on personal success · 1883–1970

Napoleon Hill was an American writer who grew up in the mountains of Wise County, Virginia. He began as a young reporter and magazine writer. He said that in 1908 the steel owner Andrew Carnegie suggested he study how successful people think, and that this work took him more than twenty years. His books include The Law of Success (1928) and Think and Grow Rich (1937), which became one of the best-selling books of its kind. Later he wrote Success Through a Positive Mental Attitude with W. Clement Stone.
