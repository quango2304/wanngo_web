Scott Stossel

SS

American journalist and editor · Born 1969

Scott Stossel is an American journalist and editor. He is the national editor of The Atlantic magazine and was an executive editor of The American Prospect. He has lived with severe anxiety since childhood. My Age of Anxiety (2013) mixes his own story with the history and science of anxiety.
