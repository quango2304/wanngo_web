David J. Schwartz

DJ

American motivational writer and business professor · 1927–1987

David J. Schwartz was an American writer and coach. He was a professor of marketing at Georgia State University, where he also led the marketing department. He ran training programs for executives and salespeople. Out of that work came The Magic of Thinking Big, first published in 1959, which argues that the size of your thinking shapes how much you achieve. It became one of the best known self-help books of its time.
