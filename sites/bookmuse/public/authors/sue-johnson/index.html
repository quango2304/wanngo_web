Sue Johnson

SJ

British-born Canadian psychologist and couples therapist · 1947–2024

Sue Johnson was a clinical psychologist who lived and worked in Canada. With her advisor Les Greenberg she created Emotionally Focused Therapy, a way of helping couples that is based on attachment theory. She taught at the University of Ottawa and ran a center in Ottawa that trains therapists in the method. Hold Me Tight turned her work into a guide for ordinary couples. She also wrote books for therapists.
