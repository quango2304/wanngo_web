Catherine Stothart

CS

British leadership coach and author on personality and communication

Catherine Stothart is a leadership coach and team facilitator who runs her own consulting business. She has coached managers and teams at large companies, and has worked in human resources at Ford and other firms. She lived for several years in Cairo and Rio de Janeiro, which shaped her interest in human behavior. She has served as a trustee of the British Association for Psychological Type. How to Get On with Anyone (2018) was her first book, and she later wrote Motivation: The Ultimate Guide to Leading Your Team.
