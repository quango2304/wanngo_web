Clarissa Pinkola Estés

CP

Jungian psychoanalyst, poet and storyteller

Clarissa Pinkola Estés is an American writer, poet and Jungian psychoanalyst. She calls herself a cantadora, a keeper of old stories. She earned a doctorate in ethno-clinical psychology in 1981 and a diploma as a certified Jungian analyst in 1984. Her best-known book is Women Who Run With the Wolves, published in 1992, which reads old myths and fairy tales as maps of the soul. Her other books include The Gift of Story, The Faithful Gardener and Untie the Strong Woman.
