Dale Carnegie

American writer and teacher of public speaking · 1888–1955

Dale Carnegie was an American writer and teacher who grew up on a farm in Missouri. In 1912 he began teaching a public speaking class at a YMCA in New York, and the classes grew into the Dale Carnegie Course. His book How to Win Friends and Influence People (1936) became a bestseller. He also wrote Lincoln the Unknown (1932), about Abraham Lincoln. How to Stop Worrying and Start Living came out in 1948 and gathers practical ways to deal with everyday worry.

Summaries of Dale Carnegie's books

  1. How to Win Friends and Influence People in the Digital AgeDale Carnegie & Associates · 20 minCarnegie's old rules still work online: you win friends and influence people by leaving each one a little better, and that starts with real interest in them.
  2. How to Stop Worrying And Start LivingDale Carnegie · 17 minWorry wears down your body and clouds your mind, but you can break the habit by living one day at a time, facing the worst with clear facts, and choosing calm action.
