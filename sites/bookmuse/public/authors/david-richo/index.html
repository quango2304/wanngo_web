David Richo

DR

American psychotherapist and teacher · Born 1940

David Richo is a psychotherapist and teacher who shares his time between Santa Barbara and San Francisco, California. His work draws on Jungian, mythic and spiritual ideas, and he describes himself as a therapist on a Buddhist path. How to Be an Adult in Relationships first came out in 2002, with a twentieth anniversary edition in 2021. He has written many other books, including Shadow Dance, Daring to Trust and How to Be an Adult in Love.
