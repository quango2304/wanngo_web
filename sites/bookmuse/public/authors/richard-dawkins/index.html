Richard Dawkins

British evolutionary biologist and writer · Born 1941

Richard Dawkins is a British evolutionary biologist and writer. He was born in Nairobi, Kenya, in 1941. He was the first Simonyi Professor for the Public Understanding of Science at the University of Oxford, from 1995 to 2008, and is an emeritus fellow of New College. His books include The Selfish Gene (1976), The Extended Phenotype, The Blind Watchmaker and The God Delusion.
