Bruce Patton

BP

Co-founder of the Harvard Negotiation Project and co-author of Getting to Yes

Bruce Patton is a negotiation teacher and writer. He is a co-founder and distinguished fellow of the Harvard Negotiation Project, and he was a lecturer on law at Harvard Law School for 15 years. In 1997 he and four colleagues founded Vantage Partners, a consulting firm. He co-wrote Getting to Yes, and with Sheila Heen and Douglas Stone he wrote Difficult Conversations.
