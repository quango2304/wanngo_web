Mitch Albom

MA

American author, sports columnist and radio host · Born 1958

Mitch Albom is an American writer from Michigan. He worked for many years as a sports columnist at the Detroit Free Press and also became a radio host and a TV commentator. His 1997 book Tuesdays with Morrie tells of his weekly visits to his dying college professor, and it became an international bestseller. He followed it with The Five People You Meet in Heaven. He also started charities in the Detroit area, including one that helps young people study the arts.
