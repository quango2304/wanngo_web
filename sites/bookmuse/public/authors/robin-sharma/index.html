Robin Sharma

Canadian leadership speaker and author

Robin Sharma is a Canadian writer and speaker on leadership and personal growth. He worked as a litigation lawyer before he became an author. He self-published his first book, MegaLiving, in 1994. His next book, The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari, came out in 1997 and was later picked up by HarperCollins. He founded Sharma Leadership International, a training company, and wrote The 5 AM Club in 2018.

www.robinsharma.com
