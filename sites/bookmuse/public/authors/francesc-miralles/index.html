Francesc Miralles

FM

Spanish writer of self-help books and novels · Born 1968

Francesc Miralles was born in Barcelona. He studied journalism, English literature and German, and has worked as an editor, a translator, a ghostwriter and a musician. He has written many bestselling self-help and inspirational books. His novel Love in Lowercase has been translated into twenty languages. With Héctor García he wrote Ikigai, based on a week of interviews in the village of Ogimi.
