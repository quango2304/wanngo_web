Jaron Lanier

Computer scientist, writer and composer · Born 1960

Jaron Lanier is an American computer scientist, writer and musician. He worked on virtual reality in the 1980s, and he helped make the term well known. He later worked in Silicon Valley and at Microsoft, but he has never had a social media account. His books include You Are Not a Gadget, Who Owns the Future? and Ten Arguments for Deleting Your Social Media Accounts Right Now.

www.jaronlanier.com
