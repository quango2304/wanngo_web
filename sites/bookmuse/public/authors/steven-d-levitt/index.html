Steven D. Levitt

American economist and co-author of Freakonomics · Born 1967

Steven D. Levitt is an American economist. He taught at the University of Chicago and is now a professor emeritus there. In 2003 he won the John Bates Clark Medal, a prize for the best American economist under 40, for his work on crime. He is known for using data to answer odd, everyday questions. With Stephen J. Dubner he wrote Freakonomics and its sequels.
