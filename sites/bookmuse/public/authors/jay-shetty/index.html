Jay Shetty

British author, podcaster and former monk · Born 1987

Jay Shetty is a British author, podcast host and coach. At twenty-one he joined an ashram near Mumbai and spent three years as a Hindu monk, studying the Vedas. He then began sharing what he learned through videos and podcasts, and in 2019 he created the podcast On Purpose. He wrote Think Like a Monk and then 8 Rules of Love, and he coaches individuals and couples.
