Eric Ries

American entrepreneur and author of The Lean Startup · Born 1978

Eric Ries is an American entrepreneur and writer who studied computer science at Yale University. In 2004 he co-founded the online company IMVU, where he was chief technology officer. He began writing about the lean startup method on his blog in 2008. His 2011 book The Lean Startup became a bestseller, and he followed it with The Startup Way in 2017. He also helped organize the Long-Term Stock Exchange, which the U.S. securities regulator approved in 2019.
