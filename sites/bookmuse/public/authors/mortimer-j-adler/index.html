Mortimer J. Adler

American philosopher, teacher and editor of the great books · 1902–2001

Mortimer J. Adler was an American philosopher and teacher, born in New York City. In 1930 he joined the University of Chicago, where he worked with its president, Robert Hutchins. How to Read a Book came out in 1940. He later edited Great Books of the Western World and its two-volume Syntopicon (1952) for Encyclopaedia Britannica. He also wrote The Paideia Proposal (1982), a plan for schools.
