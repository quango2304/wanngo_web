Hans Rosling

Swedish doctor, professor of global health and public speaker · 1948–2017

Hans Rosling was a Swedish doctor who became a professor of international health at the Karolinska Institute. He co-founded the Gapminder Foundation, which turns world statistics into clear, moving pictures. He gave several TED talks that used data and charts to explore how the world is developing. He died of pancreatic cancer in 2017, and Factfulness, written with his son and daughter-in-law, came out after his death.
