Walter Isaacson

American biographer and journalist · Born 1952

Walter Isaacson is an American writer who is known for biographies of people who changed their world. He was the managing editor of Time magazine and the chairman of CNN, and later led the Aspen Institute. His books include lives of Benjamin Franklin, Albert Einstein and Henry Kissinger. He wrote the biography of Steve Jobs after more than forty talks with Jobs over two years.
