Brian Tracy

BT

Canadian-American speaker and writer on success and selling · 1944–2026

Brian Tracy was a Canadian-American speaker, business trainer and author. He grew up in Canada and left school early, then worked in laboring jobs before moving into sales and later into business. He founded Brian Tracy International in 1984 and taught success and selling skills to audiences around the world. His books include Eat That Frog!, Goals! and No Excuses! The Power of Self-Discipline (2010).

www.briantracy.com
