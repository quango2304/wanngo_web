Josh Kaufman

JK

American author and business writer · Born 1980

Josh Kaufman is an American author who writes about business, learning and practical wisdom. He worked in brand management at Procter & Gamble, then left to build his own project, the Personal MBA, a self-study guide to how business works. The book grew into a bestseller read around the world. He later wrote The First 20 Hours, about learning new skills fast, and How to Fight a Hydra.
