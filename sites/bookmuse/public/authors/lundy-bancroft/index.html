Lundy Bancroft

LB

American author and consultant on domestic abuse

Lundy Bancroft is an author, workshop leader and consultant on domestic abuse and child maltreatment. He was a codirector of Emerge, described as the first program in the United States for abusive men. He spent about fifteen years working with abusive men as a counselor, evaluator and investigator before Why Does He Do That? came out in 2002. He also works as a custody evaluator and trains courts and agencies. His other books include The Batterer as Parent, written with Jay Silverman, and a first novel, In Custody.
