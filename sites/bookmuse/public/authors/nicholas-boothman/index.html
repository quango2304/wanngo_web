Nicholas Boothman

NB

English author and speaker on first impressions · Born 1946

Nicholas Boothman is an English author and speaker who lives in Canada. He worked for about 25 years as a fashion and advertising photographer. He then trained in Neuro-Linguistic Programming, a method about how people communicate, and began teaching what he learned in talks and seminars. His book How to Make People Like You in 90 Seconds or Less came out in 2000. He later wrote Convince Them in 90 Seconds or Less.
