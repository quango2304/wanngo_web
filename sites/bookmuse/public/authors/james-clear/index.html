James Clear

American writer on habits and self-improvement · Born 1986

James Clear is an American writer who grew up in Hamilton, Ohio. He studied biomechanics at Denison University, where he played baseball. In 2012 he began posting articles about habits on his website, and a weekly email newsletter grew from it. His 2018 book Atomic Habits shows how tiny daily changes add up to big results over time. The book has sold more than 30 million copies and has been translated into over 60 languages.
