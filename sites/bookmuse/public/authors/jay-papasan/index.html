Jay Papasan

American author, editor and executive coach

Jay Papasan is an American writer and editor. He studied at the University of Memphis and earned a master's degree in creative writing at New York University. He worked as an editor at HarperCollins in New York before joining Keller Williams in 2000. With Gary Keller, he has written many books, including the bestseller The ONE Thing. He now leads a company built around the book's ideas, coaches business leaders and hosts The ONE Thing podcast.

www.jaypapasan.com
