Olivia Fox Cabane

OF

French-American author, speaker and executive coach on charisma

Olivia Fox Cabane is an author and speaker who was born in Paris. She coaches business leaders on how to influence and inspire others. She created a charisma and leadership course for the business school at UC Berkeley, and she has lectured at Harvard, Yale, Stanford and MIT. Her first book, The Charisma Myth, came out in 2012. She also co-wrote The Net and the Butterfly.

www.askolivia.com
