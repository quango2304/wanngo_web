Barrie Davenport

BD

American personal coach and author on habits and relationships

Barrie Davenport is a certified personal coach and author. She runs the personal growth site Live Bold and Bloom, which has offered guidance since 2010. Her books cover habits, mindfulness, confidence and relationships, including 201 Relationship Questions and 10-Minute Mindfulness. She wrote Mindful Relationship Habits with S.J. Scott.
