Robert Waldinger

RW

American psychiatrist and director of the Harvard Study of Adult Development · Born 1951

Robert Waldinger is a psychiatrist and a professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School. He directs the Harvard Study of Adult Development, which has followed the lives of the same people since 1938. He also leads a teaching program in psychotherapy at Massachusetts General Hospital. He is a Zen teacher and a founder of the Lifespan Research Foundation. His 2015 TEDx talk on what makes a good life became one of the most watched TEDx talks. The Good Life (2023) is his book with Marc Schulz.
