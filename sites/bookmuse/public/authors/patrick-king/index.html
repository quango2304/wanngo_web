Patrick King

PK

Social skills coach and author of books on conversation and people skills

Patrick King is a social skills coach and author based in San Francisco. He worked as a lawyer before he began coaching people on how to talk and get along with others. He runs Patrick King Consulting, which offers coaching and free conversation resources. He has written many books on social skills, dating and communication, including Read People Like a Book.

www.patrickkingconsulting.com
