Neil deGrasse Tyson

American astrophysicist and science communicator · Born 1958

Neil deGrasse Tyson was born in New York City in 1958. He studied physics at Harvard and earned his Ph.D. in astrophysics at Columbia. Since 1996 he has directed the Hayden Planetarium in New York. He hosted the 2014 television series Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey, a follow-up to Carl Sagan's Cosmos, and he hosts the show and podcast StarTalk. Astrophysics for People in a Hurry (2017) is one of his best-known books.
