Jordan B. Peterson

Canadian clinical psychologist and professor · Born 1962

Jordan B. Peterson is a Canadian clinical psychologist who grew up in Fairview, Alberta. He taught at Harvard and then at the University of Toronto, and kept a clinical practice. His first book, Maps of Meaning, came out in 1999 and studies myths and belief. He began posting his lectures online in 2013, and they reached a very large audience. 12 Rules for Life, published in 2018, grew out of a list of rules he wrote for a question-and-answer website.
