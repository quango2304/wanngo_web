S.J. Scott

SS

American author on habits and personal productivity

Steve "S.J." Scott is an author who writes about building positive habits and routines. He founded the website Develop Good Habits. His books include Habit Stacking, Declutter Your Mind and 10-Minute Mindfulness. He wrote Mindful Relationship Habits with Barrie Davenport.

www.developgoodhabits.com
