Michelle Tillis Lederman

MT

American communication coach, trainer and networking author · Born 1971

Michelle Tillis Lederman is the founder and CEO of Executive Essentials, a company that offers communication and leadership training and coaching. She worked in finance before she became a coach, and she taught communication as an adjunct professor at New York University's Stern School of Business. The 11 Laws of Likability came out in 2012. She has also written Heroes Get Hired and Nail the Interview, Land the Job.

www.michelletillislederman.com
