David Allen

American author and productivity consultant · Born 1945

David Allen is an American author and productivity consultant. He was born in 1945 and grew up in Shreveport, Louisiana, where he acted and won a state debate title. He studied at New College in Sarasota, Florida, and did graduate work in American history at the University of California, Berkeley. In the 1980s he began consulting, and he later founded the David Allen Company, which trains people in his Getting Things Done method. His first book on it came out in 2001, and he has written several more, including Ready for Anything and Making It All Work.
