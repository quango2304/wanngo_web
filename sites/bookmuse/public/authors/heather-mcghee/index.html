Heather McGhee

American policy expert, former president of Demos and author of The Sum of Us · Born 1980

Heather McGhee grew up on the South Side of Chicago. She studied American studies at Yale and law at the University of California, Berkeley. She worked for the research group Demos on economic policy and became its president in 2014. She later became chair of the board of Color of Change, a racial justice group. Her 2021 book, The Sum of Us, reached number three on the New York Times nonfiction best-seller list.
