Pico Iyer

PI

English-born essayist, novelist and travel writer · Born 1957

Pico Iyer is an essayist, novelist and travel writer, born in Oxford, England, to parents of Indian descent. He studied at Eton, Oxford and Harvard and wrote for Time magazine. His books include Video Night in Kathmandu, The Lady and the Monk, The Global Soul and The Art of Stillness. He has lived in Japan since 1992 and writes full time.
