Joseph Nguyen

JN

Writer and speaker on overthinking and peace of mind

Joseph Nguyen is a writer, teacher and speaker who helps people look beyond their own thinking. His 2022 book Don't Believe Everything You Think grew out of what he learned from teachers of the Three Principles, an idea first described by Sydney Banks. He says he used it with his own coaching clients before he wrote the book. The book has been translated into more than 31 languages. He has also written The Overthinker's Guide to Making Decisions.
