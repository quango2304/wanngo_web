John Gray

JG

American relationship counselor, lecturer and author · Born 1951

John Gray is an American author and relationship counselor, born in Houston, Texas. He has taught relationship seminars for decades and wrote many books on how men and women communicate. His best known book, Men Are from Mars, Women Are from Venus, came out in 1992 and became a worldwide bestseller. He built its ideas from his counseling work and seminars. He lives in California.
