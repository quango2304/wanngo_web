Kam Knight

KK

Author on speed reading, memory and focus

Kam Knight writes self-help books about how to use the mind better. His topics include speed reading, memory, concentration and mind mapping. His books are published under the name MindLily.com. In Speed Reading he says he also teaches memory improvement. His own website lists his books and free guides.
