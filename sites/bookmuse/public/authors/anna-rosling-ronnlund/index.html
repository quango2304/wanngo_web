Anna Rosling Rönnlund

Swedish designer and co-founder of Gapminder · Born 1975

Anna Rosling Rönnlund is a Swedish designer who helped found the Gapminder Foundation in 2005 and leads its design. With her husband, Ola Rosling, she built Trendalyzer, software that shows statistics as moving charts. She created Dollar Street, a website that shows how families live at different incomes by using photos of their homes. She wrote Factfulness with Ola and his father, Hans Rosling.
