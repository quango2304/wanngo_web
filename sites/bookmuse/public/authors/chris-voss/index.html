Chris Voss

Former FBI hostage negotiator, author and negotiation teacher · Born 1957

Chris Voss is an American negotiator and writer. He spent 24 years in the FBI and became its lead international kidnapping negotiator. He retired from the FBI in 2007 and later started The Black Swan Group, a company that teaches negotiation skills. He has taught at Georgetown University and the University of Southern California. His 2016 book Never Split the Difference, written with Tahl Raz, turns his FBI methods into tools for everyday talks.
