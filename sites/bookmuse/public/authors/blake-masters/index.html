Blake Masters

BM

Venture capitalist and co-author of Zero to One · Born 1986

Blake Masters is an American investor and writer. He studied political science and then law at Stanford. While a law student he took notes in Peter Thiel's 2012 startup class, and those notes spread widely and became the base of Zero to One in 2014. He later worked as chief operating officer of Thiel Capital and as president of the Thiel Foundation. He has also run for public office in Arizona.
