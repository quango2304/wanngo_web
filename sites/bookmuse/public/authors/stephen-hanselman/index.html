Stephen Hanselman

SH

American publisher, literary agent and co-author of the Daily Stoic books

Stephen Hanselman has worked in publishing for more than thirty years, as a bookseller, publisher and literary agent. He spent years at HarperCollins and in 2005 founded the agency LevelFiveMedia. He wrote The Daily Stoic, The Daily Stoic Journal and Lives of the Stoics with Ryan Holiday, and he translated the Stoic passages in The Daily Stoic.
