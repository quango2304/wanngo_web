Steve Dalton

SD

American career coach and author · Born 1976

Steve Dalton is a career coach at Duke University's Fuqua School of Business, where he holds an MBA. Before that he was a strategy consultant at A. T. Kearney and a marketing manager at General Mills. He built a job search method for the students he coached, and it became The 2-Hour Job Search in 2012. Later he wrote The Job Closer and started a firm that trains organizations in his method.
