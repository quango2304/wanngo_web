Scott Jurek

SJ

American ultramarathon runner and author · Born 1973

Scott Jurek was born in Duluth, Minnesota, and became one of the best-known ultramarathon runners in the world. He won the Western States 100-mile race seven times in a row, from 1999 to 2005. He also won the Badwater race through Death Valley and the Spartathlon in Greece. In 2010 he set a U.S. record of 165.7 miles in 24 hours. He eats only plant-based food and wrote Eat & Run, published in 2012, with Steve Friedman.
