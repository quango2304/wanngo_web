Jeff Sutherland

JS

Co-creator of Scrum and founder of Scrum Inc. · Born 1941

Jeff Sutherland is an American computer scientist and project manager. He was a fighter pilot in Vietnam, later earned a doctorate and then ran software companies. In the early 1990s he worked out Scrum with Ken Schwaber, and in 2001 he was one of the seventeen people who wrote the Agile Manifesto. He founded the consulting firm Scrum Inc. in 2006.
