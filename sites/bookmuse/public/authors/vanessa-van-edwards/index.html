Vanessa Van Edwards

American author, speaker and people-skills researcher · Born 1985

Vanessa Van Edwards is an American author and speaker. She studied at Emory University and is the lead investigator of Science of People, a research lab on human behavior that began in 2012 as a blog. She teaches people skills through online courses, talks and corporate workshops. Her books include Captivate (2017) and Cues (2022).

www.scienceofpeople.com
