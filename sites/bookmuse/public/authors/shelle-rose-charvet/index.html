Shelle Rose Charvet

SR

Canadian communication trainer and consultant

Shelle Rose Charvet is a communication trainer, speaker and consultant based in Ontario, Canada. She taught communication seminars in Europe, where she learned Neuro-linguistic Programming in the 1980s. In 1985 she met the Language and Behaviour Profile, a tool made by Rodger Bailey, and she has spent years applying it in hiring, sales and training. Her book Words That Change Minds came out in 1995, with a second edition in 2010. She also runs courses that certify others to use the tool.

www.wordsthatchangeminds.com
