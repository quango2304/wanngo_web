Stephen Hawking

British theoretical physicist and author · 1942–2018

Stephen Hawking was born in Oxford, England, and studied at Oxford and Cambridge. He worked on black holes and the beginning of the universe, and with Roger Penrose he showed that the big bang must have started from a singularity. He found that black holes give off a faint glow, now called Hawking radiation. He held the Lucasian chair of mathematics at Cambridge, the post once held by Isaac Newton. A Brief History of Time (1988) brought modern physics to millions of readers.
