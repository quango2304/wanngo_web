Malala Yousafzai

Pakistani education activist and Nobel Peace Prize winner · Born 1997

Malala Yousafzai was born in 1997 in Mingora, in the Swat Valley of Pakistan. She spoke out for girls' right to go to school. In 2012 the Pakistani Taliban shot her on her school bus, and she survived. She co-founded the Malala Fund and in 2014 shared the Nobel Peace Prize, as the youngest winner. She studied politics, philosophy and economics at Oxford and graduated in 2020.
