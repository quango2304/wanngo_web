Morgan Housel

American writer and investor on money and behavior

Morgan Housel is an American writer on money and how people behave with it. He wrote for The Motley Fool for about nine years and later for The Wall Street Journal. He is a partner at the venture firm Collaborative Fund and lives in Seattle. His books are The Psychology of Money (2020), Same as Ever and The Art of Spending Money. He has won two Best in Business awards and the New York Times Sidney Award.
