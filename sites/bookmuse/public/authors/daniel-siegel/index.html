Daniel J. Siegel

American psychiatrist and author on the brain and relationships · Born 1957

Daniel J. Siegel is a psychiatrist who trained at Harvard Medical School and UCLA, with a focus on children and families. He is a clinical professor of psychiatry at the UCLA School of Medicine and runs the Mindsight Institute. He coined the word mindsight for seeing your own mind and the minds of others. His books include The Developing Mind, Mindsight and Parenting from the Inside Out. He wrote The Whole-Brain Child with Tina Payne Bryson.
