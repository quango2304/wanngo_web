Bill Canady

BC

American business executive and author of the profitable growth operating system

Bill Canady is a business leader with more than thirty years of experience in industrial and consumer products. He is a US Navy veteran who earned a business degree at Elmhurst University and an MBA at the University of Chicago. He has been CEO of several companies, including OTC Industrial Technologies, a company backed by private equity. He created the profitable growth operating system, a set of steps built on the 80/20 rule. He also wrote From Panic to Profit.
