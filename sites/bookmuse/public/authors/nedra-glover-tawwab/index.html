Nedra Glover Tawwab

NG

American licensed therapist and author on boundaries and relationships

Nedra Glover Tawwab is an American licensed clinical social worker and writer. She studied social work at Wayne State University, started a therapy practice in Detroit and later opened Kaleidoscope Counseling in Charlotte, North Carolina. Her Instagram posts about healthy relationships and boundaries reached a very large audience, and they led to her first book, Set Boundaries, Find Peace. She has since written Drama Free and other books on relationships.

www.nedratawwab.com
