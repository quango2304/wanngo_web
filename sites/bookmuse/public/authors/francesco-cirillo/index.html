Francesco Cirillo

FC

Software designer, teacher and creator of the Pomodoro Technique

Francesco Cirillo is a software designer, author and teacher. He came up with the Pomodoro Technique as a university student in the late 1980s, using a tomato-shaped kitchen timer to help him study. He has taught the method to individuals since 1998 and to teams since 1999. He also designs software, mentors teams and gives talks and courses.
