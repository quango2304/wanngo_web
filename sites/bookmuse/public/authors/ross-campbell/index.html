Ross Campbell

RC

American psychiatrist and parenting author

Ross Campbell was a doctor who spent many years in psychiatric medicine, with a focus on the needs of children and teenagers. He lived in Signal Mountain, Tennessee, and led seminars for parents. His books include How to Really Love Your Child, How to Really Love Your Teenager and How to Really Love Your Angry Child. He wrote The 5 Love Languages of Children with Gary Chapman.
