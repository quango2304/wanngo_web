Gary Keller

American entrepreneur and author, founder of Keller Williams Realty · Born 1957

Gary Keller is an American businessman and writer. He was born in Pasadena, Texas. In 1983 he started the real estate company Keller Williams with a partner, and it grew into one of the largest real estate companies in North America. He first wrote books to teach real estate agents, such as The Millionaire Real Estate Agent. His 2013 book The ONE Thing, written with Jay Papasan, became a bestseller.

www.the1thing.com
