Laura Vanderkam

American writer and speaker on time management · Born 1978

Laura Vanderkam is an American writer and speaker who studies how people use their time. Her first time management book, 168 Hours, came out in 2010. She also wrote All the Money in the World, and her articles have appeared in the Wall Street Journal and USA Today. The title of this book began as a 2011 blog post of hers, and it grew into three short e-books on mornings, weekends and work. She lives near Philadelphia with her husband and their children.
