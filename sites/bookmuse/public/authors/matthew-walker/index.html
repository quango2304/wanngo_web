Matthew Walker

British neuroscientist and sleep researcher · Born 1973

Matthew Walker is a British scientist who grew up in Liverpool. He studied neuroscience at the University of Nottingham and earned a PhD in neurophysiology at Newcastle University. He is a professor of neuroscience and psychology at the University of California, Berkeley, where he founded the Center for Human Sleep Science. His 2017 book Why We Sleep was his first for a general audience.

www.sleepdiplomat.com
