Henrik Fexeus

Swedish mentalist, author and lecturer on communication · Born 1971

Henrik Fexeus is a Swedish mentalist, writer and speaker. A mentalist mixes psychology with showmanship to seem to read minds. He studied communication skills such as hypnosis, acting and magic. His book The Art of Reading Minds first came out in Sweden in 2007 and was later published in English. He has also made television shows and has written other books about people and thinking.
