Rebecca Skloot

RS

American science writer and author of The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks · Born 1972

Rebecca Skloot is an American science writer. She studied biological sciences at Colorado State University and earned a master's degree in creative nonfiction from the University of Pittsburgh. Her first book, The Immortal Life of Henrietta Lacks, came out in 2010 and reached number one on the New York Times bestseller list. She started The Henrietta Lacks Foundation, which she leads as president, and she co-edited The Best American Science Writing 2011.
