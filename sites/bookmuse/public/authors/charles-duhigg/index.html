Charles Duhigg

American journalist and author · Born 1974

Charles Duhigg is an American journalist who reported for The New York Times and later wrote for The New Yorker. He shared a Pulitzer Prize in 2013 for the Times series on Apple and the global economy. His first book, The Power of Habit, came out in 2012 and grew out of his reporting on how people and companies change. He later wrote Smarter Faster Better, about productivity.
