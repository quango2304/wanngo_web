Lane Moore

LM

American comedian, writer, actor and musician

Lane Moore is an American comedian, writer, actor and musician who lives in New York. She created and hosts the live comedy show Tinder Live, and she fronts the band It Was Romance. She wrote for The Onion, and she was the sex and relationships editor at Cosmopolitan, where she won a GLAAD Award. How to Be Alone, published in 2018, is her first book. She later wrote a second book, You Will Find Your People.
