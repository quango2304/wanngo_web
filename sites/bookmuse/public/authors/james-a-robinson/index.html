James A. Robinson

Economist at the University of Chicago and co-author of Why Nations Fail · Born 1960

James A. Robinson is an economist born in Chelmsford, England. He studied at the London School of Economics, then at the University of Warwick, and earned his PhD at Yale in 1993. Since 2015 he has been a professor at the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago. In 2012 he and Daron Acemoglu published Why Nations Fail, and in 2024 they shared the Nobel Prize in economics with Simon Johnson.

Summaries of James A. Robinson's books

  1. Why Nations FailDaron Acemoglu and James A. Robinson · 22 minNations are rich or poor because of their institutions, the rules that decide who can work, invest and hold power. Rules that share power make a country rich. Rules that let a few take from the many keep it poor.
