Peter Hollins

PH

Author and researcher on psychology and peak human performance

Peter Hollins is an author and researcher who writes about psychology and how people perform at their best. He has a degree in psychology and a graduate degree, and he is based in Seattle. His interest in self-discipline began in college, when a roommate helped him gain weight. He has written many self-help books, including Finish What You Start and The Science of Self-Learning.
