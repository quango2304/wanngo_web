Sarah Stanley Fallaw

SS

American industrial psychologist and founder of DataPoints

Sarah Stanley Fallaw is an industrial psychologist, which means she studies how people behave at work. She earned her degrees at the University of Georgia, including a doctorate in applied psychology in 2003. She joined the Affluent Market Institute, the research group her father, Thomas J. Stanley, founded. She also founded DataPoints, a firm that builds tools to measure financial behavior. After her father died in 2015, she finished The Next Millionaire Next Door, the book they had planned together.
