Fumitake Koga

FK

Japanese writer and co-author of The Courage to Be Disliked · Born 1973

Fumitake Koga is a Japanese writer. In 1999 he read Ichiro Kishimi's introduction to Adlerian psychology and was so moved by it that he read everything he could find on Alfred Adler. In 2010 he met Kishimi in Kyoto and offered to be his writer. Over several years of talks with Kishimi he shaped their ideas into the dialogue The Courage to Be Disliked, first published in Japan in 2013.
