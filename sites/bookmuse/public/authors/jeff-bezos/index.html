Jeff Bezos

American entrepreneur, founder of Amazon · Born 1964

Jeff Bezos was born in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and studied engineering at Princeton University. He founded Amazon in 1994, first as an online bookstore, and led it as chief executive until 2021, when Andy Jassy took over. He also founded the space company Blue Origin in 2000 and bought the Washington Post in 2013. Since 1997 he has written a yearly letter to Amazon's shareholders, and this book collects those letters with some of his talks and interviews.
