Randall Munroe

RM

American cartoonist and author · Born 1984

Randall Munroe is an American cartoonist and author. He studied physics and then worked on robotics at NASA. He left to draw xkcd, a stick-figure webcomic about science, math and daily life, full time. His website's What If? series answers odd questions from readers with real math, and it became the 2014 book What If?. He has also written Thing Explainer and How To.
