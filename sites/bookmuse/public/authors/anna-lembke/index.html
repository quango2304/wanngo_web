Anna Lembke

American psychiatrist and addiction medicine specialist · Born 1967

Anna Lembke is a psychiatrist and a professor at Stanford University School of Medicine, where she is medical director of addiction medicine. She trained in medicine and psychiatry at Stanford and treats people with addiction. Her first book, Drug Dealer, MD (2016), looks at how doctors helped start the opioid epidemic. In Dopamine Nation (2021) she uses her patients' stories and brain science to explain how to find balance in a world of easy rewards.
