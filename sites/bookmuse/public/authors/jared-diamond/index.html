Jared Diamond

American scientist and professor at UCLA, author of Guns, Germs, and Steel · Born 1937

Jared Diamond is an American scientist who trained in physiology and later studied bird evolution and ecology. He has done fieldwork in New Guinea for decades. He is a professor of geography at the University of California, Los Angeles. His 1997 book Guns, Germs, and Steel won the Pulitzer Prize for general nonfiction. He has also written The Third Chimpanzee, Collapse and Upheaval.
