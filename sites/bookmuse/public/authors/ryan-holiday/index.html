Ryan Holiday

American author and marketer who writes about Stoicism · Born 1987

Ryan Holiday was born in Sacramento, California, in 1987. He left college early and started his career in marketing, including a job at American Apparel. He turned to writing and is best known for books that bring Stoic ideas to everyday readers, such as The Obstacle Is the Way, Ego Is the Enemy and Stillness Is the Key. He wrote The Daily Stoic and Lives of the Stoics with Stephen Hanselman, and he hosts the Daily Stoic podcast.
