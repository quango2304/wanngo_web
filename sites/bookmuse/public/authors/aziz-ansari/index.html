Aziz Ansari

American stand-up comedian, actor and writer · Born 1983

Aziz Ansari is an American comedian and actor from South Carolina. He became well known for playing Tom Haverford on the TV series Parks and Recreation. He later co-created and starred in the series Master of None. His stand-up shows were the starting point for Modern Romance, which he wrote in 2015 with the sociologist Eric Klinenberg.
