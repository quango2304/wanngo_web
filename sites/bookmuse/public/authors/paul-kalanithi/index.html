Paul Kalanithi

American neurosurgeon and writer · 1977–2015

Paul Kalanithi was an American neurosurgeon and writer who grew up in Kingman, Arizona. He studied English literature and human biology at Stanford, then history and philosophy of science and medicine at Cambridge, and finished medical school at Yale. He trained as a neurosurgeon at Stanford. At thirty-six, near the end of his training, he was diagnosed with stage IV lung cancer. He wrote When Breath Becomes Air while he was ill, and it was published after he died in March 2015.
