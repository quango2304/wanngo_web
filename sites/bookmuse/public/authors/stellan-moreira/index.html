Stellan Moreira

SM

Self-help author of Million Dollar Habits

Stellan Moreira is named as the author of Million Dollar Habits: 27 Powerful Habits to Wire Your Mind for Success, Become Truly Happy, and Achieve Financial Freedom. The book's own pages credit Simpol, a writing team that describes itself as two young entrepreneurs. They say they are self-educated and want to help readers find their purpose. The book carries a 2017 copyright for SimPol Publishing. This is a different book from Brian Tracy's of the same title.
