Iben Dissing Sandahl

ID

Danish psychotherapist and author on children and families

Iben Dissing Sandahl is a Danish narrative psychotherapist and coach with a practice near Copenhagen, where she counsels families and children. She first trained as a teacher and worked for ten years in the Danish school system. She wrote The Danish Way of Parenting with Jessica Joelle Alexander. Her other books include The Danish Way of Raising Teens and Play The Danish Way.
