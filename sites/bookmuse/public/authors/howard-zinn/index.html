Howard Zinn

HZ

American historian, professor and political activist · 1922–2010

Howard Zinn was an American historian who grew up in a working-class family in New York. He served as a bombardier in the Army Air Forces in World War II, then studied history on the GI Bill and earned a PhD at Columbia University. He taught at Spelman College in Atlanta from 1956 to 1963, and later at Boston University. He took part in the civil rights movement and the protests against the Vietnam War. His best known book, A People's History of the United States, came out in 1980. He died in 2010.
