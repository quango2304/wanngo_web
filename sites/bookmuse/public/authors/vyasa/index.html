Vyasa

V

Sage named by tradition as the author of the Mahabharata and the Gita

Vyasa is a sage of Indian tradition, said to be the author of the Mahabharata, the great epic that holds the Bhagavad Gita. The Gita's own closing lines credit his learning. He also appears as a character inside the epic. Nothing sure is known about his life or dates, and scholars think the epic grew over many centuries. Because of this, he is best seen as a traditional name for its author.

Summaries of Vyasa's books

  1. The Bhagavad GitaVyasa · 17 minYou are a deathless self, so do your duty with a steady mind, let go of the reward, and make God your refuge.
