Christina Lamb

British journalist and author, chief foreign correspondent of The Sunday Times · Born 1965

Christina Lamb is a British journalist and author. She is the chief foreign correspondent of The Sunday Times, and earlier worked for the Financial Times, based in Islamabad and Rio de Janeiro. She has written ten books. I Am Malala (2013), written with Malala Yousafzai, was named Popular Non-Fiction Book of the Year at the 2013 British National Book Awards. She was made an OBE in 2013.
