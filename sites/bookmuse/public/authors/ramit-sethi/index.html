Ramit Sethi

American author, entrepreneur and personal finance teacher · Born 1982

Ramit Sethi is an American author and entrepreneur who teaches personal finance. He began writing a blog called I Will Teach You to Be Rich in 2004, while he was at Stanford. The blog grew into his first book of the same name, published in 2009, and he updated it in a second edition in 2019. He also hosts a podcast and the 2023 Netflix series How to Get Rich. He co-founded the wiki company PBworks.
