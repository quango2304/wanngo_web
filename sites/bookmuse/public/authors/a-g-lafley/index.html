A.G. Lafley

Former chief executive of Procter & Gamble and business author · Born 1947

A.G. Lafley is an American business leader who spent his career at Procter & Gamble. He led the company as chief executive from 2000 to 2010, and again from 2013 to 2015, and then served as its executive chairman until 2016. He studied at Hamilton College and Harvard Business School. He wrote The Game Changer with Ram Charan in 2008, and Playing to Win with Roger Martin in 2013.
