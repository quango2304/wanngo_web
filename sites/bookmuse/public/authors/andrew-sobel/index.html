Andrew Sobel

AS

American author and adviser on client relationships

Andrew Sobel writes and teaches about how to build lasting relationships with clients. He was a senior partner and country chief executive at a large global consulting firm before starting his own company. He has coached and trained professionals around the world. His books include Clients for Life, Making Rain, All for One and Power Questions.
