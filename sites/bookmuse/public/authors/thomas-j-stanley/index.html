Thomas J. Stanley

TJ

American marketing professor and researcher of the wealthy · 1944–2015

Thomas J. Stanley was born in New York and earned a doctorate in business administration from the University of Georgia in 1974. He taught marketing at the University at Albany and then at Georgia State University. In 1989 he left teaching to do research, consulting and speaking full time. He spent years surveying and interviewing wealthy Americans, and wrote several books about them. The Millionaire Next Door, written with William D. Danko, spent more than 170 weeks on the New York Times best seller list. He died in Georgia in 2015, struck by an impaired driver.
