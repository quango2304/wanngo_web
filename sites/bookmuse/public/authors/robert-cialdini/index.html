Robert B. Cialdini

Social psychologist and author on persuasion · Born 1945

Robert Cialdini is an American social psychologist. He is Regents' Professor Emeritus of Psychology and Marketing at Arizona State University. To learn how people are persuaded, he combined lab experiments with time spent undercover among salespeople, fundraisers and marketers. This work led to his 1984 book Influence, which has been updated several times. He also founded the firm Influence At Work.
