David Bach

American financial author and speaker · Born 1966

David Bach is an American author and speaker on personal finance. He began as a financial advisor in the early 1990s and became a senior vice president at Morgan Stanley before leaving in 2001 to write and teach full time. He founded FinishRich Media for his books and seminars. His Finish Rich books have sold more than seven million copies. The Automatic Millionaire and Start Late, Finish Rich were both number one New York Times bestsellers.
