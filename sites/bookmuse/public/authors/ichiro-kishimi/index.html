Ichiro Kishimi

IK

Japanese philosopher of Greek philosophy and Adlerian psychology · Born 1956

Ichiro Kishimi is a Japanese philosopher who lives in Kyoto. He has studied philosophy since his teens, with a focus on Greek thought. In his thirties he met the psychology of Alfred Adler and began to study it side by side with philosophy. He has also worked as a counselor at psychiatry clinics. With the writer Fumitake Koga he wrote The Courage to Be Disliked, first published in Japan in 2013.
