Wallace D. Wattles

WD

American writer on success and thought · 1860–1911

Wallace Delois Wattles was an American writer of the early 1900s. He wrote short, plain books about getting rich, being well and being great, and he published them in 1910. The best known is The Science of Getting Rich, which later writers on success and the law of attraction have often named as a source. He died in 1911, not long after his books appeared.

Summaries of Wallace D. Wattles's books

  1. The Science of Getting RichWallace D. Wattles · 20 minWallace D. Wattles claims that getting rich is an exact science: picture what you want, hold it with faith and thanks, and act well every day, and you will get rich.
