J.D. Vance

American writer, lawyer and politician · Born 1984

J.D. Vance grew up in Middletown, Ohio, in a family from the hills of eastern Kentucky. He served in the Marine Corps, including a tour in Iraq, then studied at Ohio State University and Yale Law School. He wrote Hillbilly Elegy, a memoir about his family and the working-class region he came from, in 2016. He later represented Ohio in the U.S. Senate and became Vice President of the United States in 2025.
