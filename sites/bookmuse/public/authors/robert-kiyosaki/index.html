Robert Kiyosaki

American investor, educator and author of Rich Dad Poor Dad · Born 1947

Robert Kiyosaki was born in Hilo, Hawaii. He graduated from the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy in 1969 and then flew as a Marine Corps pilot in the Vietnam War. After the war he worked in sales at Xerox and started his own businesses. In 1997 he published Rich Dad Poor Dad, first on his own, and it became one of the best-selling personal finance books. He and his wife Kim also created the CASHFLOW board games and the Rich Dad Company to teach financial education.

www.richdad.com
