Nixaly Leonardo

NL

Licensed clinical social worker and psychotherapist

Nixaly Leonardo, LCSW, grew up in New York City with immigrant parents. She found her calling while volunteering at a youth center, where she listened to young people who had been through trauma and loss. She earned her master's degree in social work at Fordham University and became a licensed clinical social worker. She has over 10 years of experience in mental health. In private practice she offers therapy, life coaching, parent coaching and couples counseling.
