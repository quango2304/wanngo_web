Simon Sinek

British-American writer and speaker on leadership · Born 1973

Simon Sinek is a writer and speaker on leadership, born in London. He studied cultural anthropology in college and later worked in marketing and advertising before starting his own consulting business. In 2009 he wrote Start with Why, which grew from his idea called the Golden Circle. His TEDx talk on the same idea became one of the most watched talks on TED.com. He has since written Leaders Eat Last, Together Is Better and The Infinite Game.
