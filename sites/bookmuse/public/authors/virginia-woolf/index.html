Virginia Woolf

English novelist and essayist of the modernist movement · 1882–1941

Virginia Woolf was born in London in 1882. She wrote modernist novels such as Mrs Dalloway and To the Lighthouse, and many essays. With her husband, Leonard Woolf, she started the Hogarth Press, which published her books. She belonged to the Bloomsbury Group, a circle of writers and artists in London. A Room of One's Own grew out of two talks she gave to women students at Cambridge in October 1928. She died in 1941.
