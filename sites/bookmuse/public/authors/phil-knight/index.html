Phil Knight

American businessman and co-founder of Nike · Born 1938

Phil Knight was born in Portland, Oregon. He ran track at the University of Oregon and earned an MBA at Stanford. In 1964 he and his old coach Bill Bowerman started Blue Ribbon Sports, which was renamed Nike in 1971. He was Nike's chief executive until 2004 and its chairman until 2016. He wrote his memoir, Shoe Dog, in 2016. He also owns the animation studio Laika.
