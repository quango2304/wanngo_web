Stanley McChrystal

Retired U.S. Army general and leadership author · Born 1954

Stanley McChrystal was born at Fort Leavenworth, Kansas, and graduated from the United States Military Academy at West Point in 1976. He led the Joint Special Operations Command from 2003 to 2008, and his forces were credited with the 2006 killing of Abu Musab al-Zarqawi, the leader of Al Qaeda in Iraq. He then commanded U.S. and NATO forces in Afghanistan from 2009 until he left the Army in 2010. He teaches leadership at Yale and co-founded the McChrystal Group, a consulting firm, in 2011. Team of Teams, from 2015, is one of his books.
