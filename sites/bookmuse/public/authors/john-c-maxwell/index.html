John C. Maxwell

American pastor, speaker and leadership author · Born 1947

John C. Maxwell was born in Garden City, Michigan, and followed his father into the ministry as an ordained minister of the Wesleyan Church. He led churches for many years, and later became known as a speaker and writer on leadership and personal growth. In 2011 he founded a coaching and training group that certifies leadership coaches. His books include The 21 Irrefutable Laws of Leadership, Developing the Leader Within You and Failing Forward.
