J. J. Sutherland

JJ

CEO of Scrum Inc. and former NPR war producer

J. J. Sutherland is the chief executive of Scrum Inc., the firm his father Jeff Sutherland founded. Before that he was a producer and correspondent at NPR, where he covered wars and uprisings. He helped an NPR team in Cairo work in the Scrum way during the 2011 uprising. He co-wrote Scrum with his father and also wrote The Scrum Fieldbook.
