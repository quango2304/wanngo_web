Bessel van der Kolk

Dutch-born American psychiatrist and trauma researcher · Born 1943

Bessel van der Kolk was born in The Hague in the Netherlands. He studied medicine at the University of Chicago and trained as a psychiatrist at the Massachusetts Mental Health Center, part of Harvard Medical School. He is a professor of psychiatry at Boston University School of Medicine and founded the Trauma Center in Brookline, Massachusetts, in 1982. He has studied how trauma affects the brain and body, and he edited several books on the subject. The Body Keeps the Score, from 2014, is his best-known book.
