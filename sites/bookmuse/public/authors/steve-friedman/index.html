Steve Friedman

SF

American magazine writer and author

Steve Friedman is a magazine writer and author who grew up in St. Louis, Missouri, and graduated from Stanford University. He co-wrote Eat & Run with the ultrarunner Scott Jurek, who thanks him in the book as his humble cowriter and pacer. He has written for magazines such as GQ and Esquire, and is the author of the book Driving Lessons.
