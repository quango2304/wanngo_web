Niccolò Machiavelli

Italian diplomat and political thinker from Florence · 1469–1527

Niccolò Machiavelli was born in Florence and served its republic as an official and diplomat. When the Medici family returned to power in 1512, he lost his post and left public life. He wrote The Prince in 1513, a short guide on how rulers win and keep power. He also wrote the Discourses on Livy, The Art of War and a history of Florence. His name later became a word for cunning in politics.
