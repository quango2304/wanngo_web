Carol S. Dweck

American psychologist who studies motivation and mindset · Born 1946

Carol Dweck is an American psychologist. She studied psychology at Barnard College and earned her doctorate at Yale in 1972. She taught at Columbia University and is now a professor of psychology at Stanford University. Her research looks at how people's beliefs about their own abilities shape their motivation and how they cope with failure. Her book Mindset (2006) brought the ideas of the fixed and growth mindsets to a wide audience.
