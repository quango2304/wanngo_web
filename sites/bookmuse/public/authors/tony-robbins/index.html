Tony Robbins

American speaker, coach and author · Born 1960

Tony Robbins was born in North Hollywood, California. He became known for his seminars and coaching, which he has run for decades through his own companies. His books include Unlimited Power (1986) and Awaken the Giant Within (1991). Money: Master the Game (2014) came from interviews with 50 leading investors and money experts. In 2017 he co-wrote Unshakeable with Peter Mallouk.
