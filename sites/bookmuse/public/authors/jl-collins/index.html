J.L. Collins

JC

American writer, blogger and retired businessman

J.L. Collins, known as Jim, writes about investing and financial independence. He started working at 13 and began investing in 1975. Over the years he left jobs, changed careers and made many investing mistakes before he settled on simple index funds. He retired in 2011 and lives in New Hampshire. His blog, jlcollinsnh.com, grew out of letters to his daughter and led to The Simple Path to Wealth.
