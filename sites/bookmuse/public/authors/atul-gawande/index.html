Atul Gawande

Surgeon, public health researcher and writer · Born 1965

Atul Gawande is an American surgeon who works at Brigham and Women's Hospital in Boston and teaches at Harvard. He was born in Brooklyn to parents who had come from India, and he grew up in Athens, Ohio. He became a staff writer at The New Yorker in 1998 and later turned his articles and ideas into books, including Complications, Better and The Checklist Manifesto. His 2014 book Being Mortal looks at the choices people face near the end of life and became a number one New York Times bestseller. He also led global health work at USAID from 2022 to early 2025.
