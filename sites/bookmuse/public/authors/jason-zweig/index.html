Jason Zweig

JZ

Financial journalist and columnist

Jason Zweig is an American financial journalist. He began as a reporter at Forbes magazine in 1987 and later wrote for Money magazine. He is a columnist at The Wall Street Journal. He updated Benjamin Graham's The Intelligent Investor in 2003 with a new commentary after every chapter, and he wrote the book Your Money and Your Brain about how the mind affects investing.
