Robin Norwood

RN

American therapist and author of Women Who Love Too Much · Born 1945

Robin Norwood is an American licensed marriage and family therapist. She worked in addiction treatment for about fifteen years and specialized in co-alcoholism and relationship addiction. She wrote Women Who Love Too Much, which came out in 1985 and became a number one New York Times bestseller. She also wrote Letters from Women Who Love Too Much and Daily Meditations for Women Who Love Too Much.
