Viktor E. Frankl

Viennese psychiatrist and founder of logotherapy · 1905–1997

Viktor Frankl was a psychiatrist and neurologist who lived and worked in Vienna. He was imprisoned in Nazi concentration camps for three years, and he survived. In 1945 he wrote Man's Search for Meaning in nine days. He built a method called logotherapy, which holds that the search for meaning is the main drive in people. He wrote many books and taught around the world.
