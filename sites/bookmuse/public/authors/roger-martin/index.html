Roger L. Martin

Canadian strategy adviser, writer and former dean of the Rotman School · Born 1956

Roger L. Martin is a Canadian writer and adviser on strategy. He worked for the consulting firm Monitor Company, then was dean of the Rotman School of Management at the University of Toronto from 1998 to 2013. He advised A.G. Lafley on strategy at Procter & Gamble. He has written many books, including The Opposable Mind, Fixing the Game and, with Lafley, Playing to Win in 2013.
