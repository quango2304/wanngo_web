Thomas Erikson

Swedish author and behavior specialist · Born 1965

Thomas Erikson is a Swedish author, speaker and behavior specialist. He was born in Örnsköldsvik in 1965. His first novel, a crime story, came out in 2011. Surrounded by Idiots, first published in Sweden in 2014, explains the four behavior types of the DISC model and has been translated into many languages. His own site says he writes and speaks about how people communicate, and gives more than 120 talks a year.

www.surroundedbyidiots.com
