Jonathan Haidt

American social psychologist and professor at New York University · Born 1963

Jonathan Haidt is an American social psychologist who studies morality, emotion and culture. He taught at the University of Virginia until 2011 and is now a professor at New York University's Stern School of Business. His books include The Happiness Hypothesis, The Righteous Mind and, with Greg Lukianoff, The Coddling of the American Mind. In recent years he has written about how smartphones and social media changed childhood and teen mental health.
