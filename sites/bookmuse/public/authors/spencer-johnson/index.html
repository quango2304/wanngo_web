Spencer Johnson

SJ

American doctor and writer of short books about work and change · 1938–2017

Spencer Johnson was an American writer. He studied psychology at the University of Southern California and later earned a medical degree at the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland. He wrote the children's ValueTales series in the 1970s and, with Ken Blanchard, The One Minute Manager in 1982. His 1998 parable Who Moved My Cheese? sold close to 30 million copies. He died in San Diego in 2017, aged 78.
