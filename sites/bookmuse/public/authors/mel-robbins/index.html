Mel Robbins

MR

American speaker, podcaster and author · Born 1968

Mel Robbins is an American speaker, podcaster and author. She trained as a lawyer and tried several other careers before a talk about a five-second countdown went viral and made her a full-time speaker. She self-published The 5 Second Rule in 2017 and later wrote The High 5 Habit. She hosts The Mel Robbins Podcast, which began in 2022. The Let Them Theory (2024) grew from a short video about the idea, and she wrote it with her daughter Sawyer.

www.melrobbins.com

Summaries of Mel Robbins's books

  2. The Let Them TheoryMel Robbins · 19 minYou cannot control other people, so say Let Them to let go of them, then say Let Me to choose what you do next.
