Mark Goulston

American psychiatrist, business consultant and author

Mark Goulston, an American psychiatrist, worked with businesses, law firms and families as a communication coach. Early in his career he worked with suicidal and violent people, and he later trained police and FBI hostage negotiators. He taught for many years at the UCLA Neuropsychiatric Institute. He wrote Just Listen, Get Out of Your Own Way and Get Out of Your Own Way at Work.
