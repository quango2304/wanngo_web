Maxwell Maltz

MM

American plastic surgeon and self-help author · 1899–1975

Maxwell Maltz was born in New York City in 1899 and earned his medical degree at Columbia University in 1923. He trained under German plastic surgeons and worked as a cosmetic surgeon. He noticed that a new face did not always change how a patient felt, and he began to study the picture people hold of themselves. He set out his ideas in Psycho-Cybernetics, first published in 1960, and he died in 1975.
