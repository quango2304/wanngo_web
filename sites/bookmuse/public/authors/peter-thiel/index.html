Peter Thiel

Entrepreneur and venture capitalist, co-founder of PayPal and Palantir · Born 1967

Peter Thiel is a German-born American businessman and investor. In 1998 he co-founded PayPal with Max Levchin and Luke Nosek, and in 2003 he helped start the data company Palantir. In 2004 he put money into Facebook and became its first outside investor. In 2005 he co-founded the venture firm Founders Fund. Zero to One, written with Blake Masters, grew from a startup class he taught at Stanford.
