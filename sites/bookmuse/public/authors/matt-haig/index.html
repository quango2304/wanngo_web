Matt Haig

English novelist and writer for children · Born 1975

Matt Haig is a British writer of novels for adults and for young readers. In his twenties he lived through severe depression and panic attacks, and he later wrote about it openly. Reasons to Stay Alive (2015) is his true account of that time and of how he recovered. His novels include The Humans, The Radleys and How to Stop Time. His books have been published in over forty languages.
