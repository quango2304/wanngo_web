Carla Naumburg

CN

American clinical social worker and parenting author

Carla Naumburg is a licensed clinical social worker in Massachusetts. She studied psychology at Middlebury College and social work at Smith College, and she has a PhD in clinical social work from Simmons College. She also trained in mindfulness and psychotherapy. She writes about parenting, stress and self-compassion, and she lives with her husband and two daughters. Her books include How to Stop Losing Your Sh*t with Your Kids and You Are Not a Sh*tty Parent.
