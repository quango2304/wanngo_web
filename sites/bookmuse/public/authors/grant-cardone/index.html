Grant Cardone

American sales trainer, author and real estate investor · Born 1958

Grant Cardone is an American businessman, speaker and author who was born in Lake Charles, Louisiana. He studied accounting at McNeese State University and built his name teaching sales. He founded Cardone Training Technologies and later Cardone Capital, a real estate investment firm. His books include Sell to Survive, If You're Not First, You're Last and The 10X Rule.
