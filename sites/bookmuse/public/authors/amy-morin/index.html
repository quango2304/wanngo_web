Amy Morin

Psychotherapist and author on mental strength

Amy Morin is an American psychotherapist and licensed clinical social worker. After she lost her mother and, a few years later, her husband, she looked for what builds mental strength and found that dropping unhelpful habits matters as much as building good ones. She wrote down a list of those habits as a letter to herself and put it online, where it spread widely. That list became her book 13 Things Mentally Strong People Don't Do (2014). She has written more books on mental strength and hosts a podcast called Mentally Stronger.
