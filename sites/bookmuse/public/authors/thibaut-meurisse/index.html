Thibaut Meurisse

Writer and coach on personal development and productivity

Thibaut Meurisse is a writer and personal development coach. He started a blog called What Is Personal Development in 2014 and wrote his first book in 2015. Since then he has written more than twenty books, including the Mastery Series, which starts with Master Your Emotions. Dopamine Detox (2021) is the first book in his Productivity Series. It explains how cutting back on quick-reward habits, such as scrolling social media, can help you focus on work that matters.
