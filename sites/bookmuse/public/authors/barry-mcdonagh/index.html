Barry McDonagh

Irish author and anxiety coach

Barry McDonagh is the author of Panic Away and Dare. He has a degree from University College Dublin and a higher diploma in psychology from the Open University, and he says he is not a clinical psychologist or a doctor. He had his first panic attack at eighteen and then worked his own way out of anxiety. He has coached people with anxiety for over ten years and created the DARE Response, a four-step method he describes in Dare.

www.dareresponse.com
