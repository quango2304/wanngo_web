Robert Greene

American author of books on power, strategy and human nature · Born 1959

Robert Greene is an American author born in Los Angeles. Before he wrote books he did many jobs, including magazine editor and Hollywood writer. In 1995 he worked at an art and media school in Italy, where he met the book producer Joost Elffers. Together they made The 48 Laws of Power (1998), which gathers lessons on power from history. He has since written The Art of Seduction, The 33 Strategies of War, Mastery and The Laws of Human Nature.

www.powerseductionandwar.com

Summaries of Robert Greene's books

  1. MasteryRobert Greene · 18 minMastery is not a gift some people are born with. It grows in anyone who finds the work that fits them, then spends years in three phases of learning, until they can feel their whole field at once.
  2. The Laws of Human NatureRobert Greene · 22 minDeep forces such as emotion, self-love, envy and fear drive us without our knowing, and once you see them in others and in yourself, you stop being caught off guard.
  3. The 48 Laws of PowerRobert Greene · 19 minPower is a game that everyone plays, and Greene says you do better when you see how it works: keep a calm face, hide your aims, manage your image, learn what people want, stop in time, and stay hard to pin down.
  4. The Art of SeductionRobert Greene · 20 minSeduction is a game of the mind, not of looks. A seducer builds a strong character, learns what the other person lacks, and leads them slowly into a spell.
