Malcolm Gladwell

Journalist and author of books on how people think · Born 1963

Malcolm Gladwell was born in England and grew up in Canada. He has been a staff writer at The New Yorker since 1996. His books look at ordinary ideas in a new way, and they include The Tipping Point (2000), Blink (2005), Outliers (2008) and David and Goliath (2013). Talking to Strangers (2019) asks why we so often misjudge people we do not know. He also hosts the podcast Revisionist History.
