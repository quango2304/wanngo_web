Leil Lowndes

LL

American author and communication coach

Leil Lowndes is an American author and speaker on how people connect. She grew up in Washington, D.C., and once worked as a cruise director on a ship. She gives seminars and has advised large companies. Her books include How to Talk to Anyone, How to Make Anyone Like You and How to Feel Confident.
