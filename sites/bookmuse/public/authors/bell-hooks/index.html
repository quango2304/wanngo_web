bell hooks

American writer, professor and social critic · 1952–2021

bell hooks was the pen name of Gloria Jean Watkins, who was born in Hopkinsville, Kentucky. She taught English at Yale University, Oberlin College and City College of New York, and later at Berea College in Kentucky. She wrote about race, gender, class and love. Her books include Ain't I a Woman? (1981), Bone Black (1996) and All About Love (2000). She died in 2021 at the age of 69.
