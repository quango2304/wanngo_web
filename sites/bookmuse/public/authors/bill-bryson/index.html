Bill Bryson

American-British writer of travel, language and science books · Born 1951

Bill Bryson was born in Des Moines, Iowa, and has lived much of his life in England. He first became known for funny travel books such as The Lost Continent and Notes from a Small Island. For A Short History of Nearly Everything (2003) he spent about three years reading science and talking to experts. It won major science-book prizes in Britain. He has also written about language, Shakespeare and the home.
