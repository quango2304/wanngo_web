Chris Fussell

CF

Former Navy SEAL officer, consultant and author

Chris Fussell was commissioned as a Navy officer in 1997 and spent about fifteen years on SEAL teams, including the Naval Special Warfare Development Group. He was General McChrystal's aide-de-camp during McChrystal's final year at the Joint Special Operations Command. He joined the McChrystal Group in 2012 and also teaches at Yale's Jackson Institute. He co-wrote Team of Teams and later books on organizations.
