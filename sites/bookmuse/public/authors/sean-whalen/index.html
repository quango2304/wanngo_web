Sean Whalen

SW

American entrepreneur, coach and author

Sean Whalen is an author, speaker and coach who founded Lions Not Sheep and a mastermind group called The Lions Den. He worked in real estate and says he built and lost a large business before rebuilding his life. He has said his coaching rests on one daily system, Core 4. How to Make Sh*t Happen, his best-known book, came out in 2018.
