William H. McRaven

Retired U.S. Navy admiral and former Navy SEAL · Born 1955

William H. McRaven is a retired four-star admiral in the U.S. Navy. He served for more than thirty-seven years, most of them as a Navy SEAL, and he led the U.S. Special Operations Command. After the Navy he became chancellor of the University of Texas System. In 2014 he gave a graduation speech at the University of Texas at Austin, built on ten lessons from SEAL training. The speech spread widely and became his book Make Your Bed (2017).
