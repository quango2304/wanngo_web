David Goggins

Retired Navy SEAL, ultra-endurance athlete and speaker · Born 1975

David Goggins grew up in Buffalo, New York, and then in small-town Indiana. He served in the U.S. Air Force and then the Navy, and became a Navy SEAL. He has run more than sixty very long races, including Badwater 135, and set a world record for pull-ups in a day. After he retired from the Navy he worked as a wildland firefighter and a public speaker. Can't Hurt Me is his first book.
