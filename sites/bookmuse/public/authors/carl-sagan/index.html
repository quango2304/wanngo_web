Carl Sagan

American astronomer and science writer · 1934–1996

Carl Sagan grew up in Brooklyn, New York, and became an astronomer and planetary scientist. He taught at Cornell University for nearly thirty years and played an active part in NASA's Mariner, Viking and Voyager missions. He helped make the gold record that travels on the two Voyager spacecraft. He won a Pulitzer Prize for his book The Dragons of Eden. With Ann Druyan he wrote and narrated the television series Cosmos in 1980, and wrote this book beside it.
