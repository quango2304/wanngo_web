Thomas Piketty

French economist who studies wealth and inequality · Born 1971

Thomas Piketty is a French economist born in 1971. He teaches at the Paris School of Economics and the School for Advanced Studies in the Social Sciences. With colleagues such as Anthony Atkinson and Emmanuel Saez, he built the World Top Incomes Database, which holds long records of income and wealth from many countries. His book Capital in the Twenty-First Century, published in French in 2013 and in English in 2014, drew on this work and reached a wide public.
