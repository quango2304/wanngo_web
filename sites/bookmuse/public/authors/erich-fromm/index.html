Erich Fromm

EF

German-American psychoanalyst and social thinker · 1900–1980

Erich Fromm was born in Frankfurt, Germany. He earned a doctorate in sociology at Heidelberg in 1922 and trained as a psychoanalyst. After the Nazis took power he left Germany and, in 1934, began work at Columbia University in New York. Later he lived in Mexico and then in Switzerland, where he died. He wrote Escape from Freedom (1941), The Sane Society (1955) and The Art of Loving (1956).
