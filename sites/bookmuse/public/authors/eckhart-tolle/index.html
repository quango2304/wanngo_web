Eckhart Tolle

German-born spiritual teacher and author · Born 1948

Eckhart Tolle is a spiritual teacher and writer who was born in Germany. He studied at the University of London and then began a doctorate at Cambridge, which he did not finish. He says that at 29 he went through a deep inner change that ended years of anxiety and depression. He later gave talks and private sessions to small groups, and these became The Power of Now (1997). He followed it with Stillness Speaks (2003) and A New Earth (2005).
