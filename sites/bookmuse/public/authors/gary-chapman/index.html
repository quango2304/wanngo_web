Gary Chapman

American author, counselor and pastor · Born 1938

Gary Chapman is an American author and counselor who was born in North Carolina in 1938. He joined the staff of Calvary Baptist Church in Winston-Salem in 1971, and his career has focused on marriage counseling and enrichment. His first book on love languages, The Five Love Languages, came out in 1992 and was written for couples. Parents then asked for a version about children, so he wrote this book with Ross Campbell in 1997.

www.5lovelanguages.com
