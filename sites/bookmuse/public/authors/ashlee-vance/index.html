Ashlee Vance

American technology journalist and author

Ashlee Vance is a technology journalist and author. He was born in South Africa, grew up mostly in Texas and studied at Pomona College. He wrote for The Register and The New York Times, then for Bloomberg Businessweek. His books include Geek Silicon Valley (2007), the Elon Musk biography (2015, with a young readers' edition in 2017) and When the Heavens Went on Sale (2023).
