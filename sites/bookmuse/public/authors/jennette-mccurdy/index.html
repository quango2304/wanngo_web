Jennette McCurdy

American writer, director and former child actor · Born 1992

Jennette McCurdy is an American writer and director from California. As a child and teenager she acted on television, most of all in the Nickelodeon shows iCarly and Sam & Cat. She stopped acting in 2017 and began to write and direct. She turned her one-woman show into the memoir I'm Glad My Mom Died. She also hosts a podcast called Empty Inside.
