Hal Elrod

American author, speaker and success coach · Born 1979

Hal Elrod is an American author, keynote speaker and coach. At 20 he was hit head-on by a drunk driver and was clinically dead for six minutes. He later built a career in sales, then in speaking and coaching. After losing his income and falling into debt and depression, he created a morning routine for himself. He shared it in The Miracle Morning, which became a bestseller and led to a series of related books.
