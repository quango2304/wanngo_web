Emeran Mayer

German-born gastroenterologist and neuroscientist · Born 1950

Emeran Mayer is a doctor and researcher who studies how the brain and the gut talk to each other. He is a professor of medicine, physiology and psychiatry at UCLA. He directs the Oppenheimer Center for Neurobiology of Stress and Resilience, and he founded the Goodman-Luskin Microbiome Center there. His research covers the gut microbiome, stress and irritable bowel syndrome. The Mind-Gut Connection (2016) was his first book for general readers.
