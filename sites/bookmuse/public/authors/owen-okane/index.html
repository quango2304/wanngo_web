Owen O’Kane

OO

Psychotherapist and author, former NHS Clinical Lead for Mental Health

Owen O’Kane is a psychotherapist who grew up in Belfast during the Troubles. He first worked as a nurse and in palliative care, and later became the NHS Clinical Lead for Mental Health in London. Today he runs a private therapy practice and gives talks about mental wellness. His first book, Ten to Zen, was a Sunday Times bestseller. He followed it with Ten Times Happier and How to Be Your Own Therapist.

www.owenokane.com
