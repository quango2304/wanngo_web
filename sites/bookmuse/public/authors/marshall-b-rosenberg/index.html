Marshall B. Rosenberg

MB

American psychologist and founder of the Center for Nonviolent Communication · 1934–2015

Marshall Rosenberg was born in Canton, Ohio, and earned a doctorate in clinical psychology at the University of Wisconsin in 1961. He grew up in Detroit and became interested in how people talk when they are in conflict. He developed a method of speaking and listening called Nonviolent Communication and taught it to families, schools, prisons and groups in conflict around the world. In 1984 he founded the Center for Nonviolent Communication, an international nonprofit. He died in 2015 at the age of 80.
