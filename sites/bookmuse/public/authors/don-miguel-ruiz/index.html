Don Miguel Ruiz

DM

Mexican author and teacher of Toltec wisdom · Born 1952

Don Miguel Ruiz is a Mexican writer and teacher who trained as a medical doctor. He teaches what he calls Toltec wisdom, a way of life he says comes from an old tradition of southern Mexico. His first book, The Four Agreements, came out in 1997 and became a bestseller. He has also written The Mastery of Love and The Voice of Knowledge, and The Fifth Agreement with his son, don Jose Ruiz.
