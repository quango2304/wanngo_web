Richard P. Feynman

American theoretical physicist and teacher · 1918–1988

Richard Feynman was an American physicist, born in New York City. He studied at MIT and Princeton and worked on the atomic bomb at Los Alamos during World War II. He taught at Cornell and then at Caltech, and shared the 1965 Nobel Prize in Physics for his work on quantum electrodynamics. His Caltech lectures became the textbook The Feynman Lectures on Physics. Late in life he served on the commission that investigated the Challenger space shuttle disaster.
