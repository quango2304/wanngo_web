Cal Newport

Computer scientist and author on focus and work · Born 1982

Cal Newport is an American computer scientist and writer. He earned his PhD at MIT and is now a professor of computer science at Georgetown University. He writes about how people can focus, work well and live a good life in a world full of digital distraction. His books include So Good They Can't Ignore You, Deep Work, Digital Minimalism and A World Without Email.
