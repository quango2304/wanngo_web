David Silverman

DS

Former Navy SEAL and co-founder of the McChrystal Group

David Silverman graduated from the U.S. Naval Academy in 1998 and became a Navy SEAL. He fought in Iraq, then served with General McChrystal in Afghanistan in 2009. He co-founded the McChrystal Group, a leadership consulting firm, with McChrystal, and co-wrote Team of Teams.
