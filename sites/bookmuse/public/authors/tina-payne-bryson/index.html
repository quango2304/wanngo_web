Tina Payne Bryson

TP

American child psychotherapist and parenting author

Tina Payne Bryson is a child and adolescent psychotherapist in Arcadia, California, who also gives parenting consultations. She earned her Ph.D. at the University of Southern California, where she studied attachment and child-rearing. She is the director of parenting education and development at the Mindsight Institute. She writes and speaks to parents, teachers and other professionals. She wrote The Whole-Brain Child with Daniel J. Siegel.
