John Townsend

JT

American psychologist, author and leadership consultant

John Townsend is an American psychologist and author. He studied psychology at North Carolina State University and theology at Dallas Theological Seminary, and earned a PhD in clinical psychology at Biola University's Rosemead School. With Henry Cloud he co-founded the Minirth-Meier Clinic West and was its clinical co-director for ten years. He wrote Boundaries with Cloud in 1992. In 2015 he founded the Townsend Institute for Leadership and Counseling at Concordia University Irvine.
