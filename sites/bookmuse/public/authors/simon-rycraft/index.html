Simon Rycraft

SR

Negotiation trainer, business adviser and author

Simon Rycraft is an English-born negotiation trainer and business adviser who lives in Austin, Texas. He has worked with startups, private equity firms and large companies on profit improvement and negotiation strategy. Earlier in his career he was a management consultant and held senior operating roles at a parking and mobility company. He studied at the University of Nottingham and the Wharton School. He founded Hacks Capital, which published his book Negotiation Hacks, and he hosts the podcast Experts Uncut.
