Susan Forward

SF

American therapist and author

Susan Forward was an American therapist and author. She trained in psychiatric social work and psychology, ran a private practice and led therapy groups. She also hosted a nationally syndicated talk show on ABC Talk Radio for six years. Her best-known books are Men Who Hate Women and the Women Who Love Them and Toxic Parents. She is credited with bringing the term emotional blackmail into wide use in her 1997 book of that name, written with Donna Frazier.

Summaries of Susan Forward's books

  1. Emotional BlackmailSusan Forward with Donna Frazier · 18 minEmotional blackmail is a threat from someone close: do what I want, or you will suffer. It works through fear, obligation and guilt, and it ends when you change how you respond.
