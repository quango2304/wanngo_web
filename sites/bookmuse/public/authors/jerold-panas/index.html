Jerold Panas

JP

American fundraising consultant and author · 1928–2018

Jerold Panas, known as Jerry, was a fundraising consultant. He founded the firm Jerold Panas, Linzy & Partners, which advised many universities, hospitals and other nonprofit groups. He also helped start the Institute for Charitable Giving. He wrote many books on fundraising, including Asking and Mega Gifts, and was honored by the Association of Fundraising Professionals in 2014.
