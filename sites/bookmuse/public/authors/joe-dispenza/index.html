Joe Dispenza

JD

American chiropractor and author on the brain and change

Joe Dispenza is a chiropractor who studied biochemistry at Rutgers University and earned his chiropractic degree at Life University in Georgia. He did further study in neuroscience, brain imaging and neuroplasticity, and he runs a chiropractic clinic in Washington State. He first became widely known as one of the people featured in the film What the BLEEP Do We Know!? He wrote Evolve Your Brain, then Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself, and he teaches workshops on changing the mind.
