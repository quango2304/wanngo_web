Marcus Aurelius

Roman emperor and Stoic thinker · 121–180

Marcus Aurelius was born in Rome in 121 and was the Roman emperor from 161 until his death in 180. He lost his parents young and was raised by his grandfather. He studied with teachers of Stoicism, a philosophy of self-control and a calm mind. Much of his reign was spent fighting wars on the northern border of the empire. He wrote the notes now called Meditations for himself, in Greek, and never meant them to be published.
