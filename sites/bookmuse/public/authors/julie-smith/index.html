Dr. Julie Smith

DJ

British clinical psychologist and mental health educator

Dr. Julie Smith is a clinical psychologist from England with over ten years of experience. She ran her own private practice. Her clients often asked why nobody had told them how the mind works, so she began making short mental health videos for social media. Her audience grew to millions during the COVID-19 pandemic. Her first book, Why Has Nobody Told Me This Before? (2022), gathers those lessons into a toolbox for everyone.
