Kevin Horsley

KH

South African memory trainer and speaker

Kevin Horsley is a South African speaker and memory trainer. He grew up dyslexic and struggled at school. After he found books on memory, he took part in the World Memory Championships in 1995 and won the title International Grandmaster of Memory. He has written four books, including Unlimited Memory, and he trains people and companies in learning, focus and memory.

www.unlimitedfocus.info
