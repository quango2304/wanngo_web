Angela Duckworth

American psychologist and author · Born 1970

Angela Duckworth is an American psychologist and author. She studied neurobiology at Harvard and neuroscience at Oxford. She then worked as a management consultant and taught math in public schools before earning a PhD in psychology at the University of Pennsylvania. She is a professor there and founded the nonprofit Character Lab. She won a MacArthur Fellowship in 2013, and her 2016 book Grit became a best seller.
