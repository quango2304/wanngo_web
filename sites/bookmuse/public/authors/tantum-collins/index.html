Tantum Collins

TC

Technology policy researcher and co-author of Team of Teams

Tantum Collins studied global affairs at Yale, where General McChrystal taught him in a leadership seminar. After graduating he spent a year leading the work on Team of Teams. He then studied at Cambridge and the London School of Economics as a Marshall Scholar. He later worked at Google DeepMind and in the U.S. government on technology and national security, including artificial intelligence policy.
