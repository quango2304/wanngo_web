Yuval Noah Harari

Israeli historian and author of Sapiens · Born 1976

Yuval Noah Harari is an Israeli historian. He studied at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem and earned his doctorate at the University of Oxford in 2002. He teaches history at the Hebrew University. His book Sapiens came out in Hebrew in 2011 and in English in 2014. He later wrote Homo Deus and 21 Lessons for the 21st Century.

www.ynharari.com
