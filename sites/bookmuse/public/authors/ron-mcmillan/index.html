Ron McMillan

Author, speaker and cofounder of Crucial Learning

Ron McMillan earned a master's degree at Brigham Young University in 1982. He worked at the Covey Leadership Center, where he met Joseph Grenny, before cofounding VitalSmarts, now Crucial Learning, in 1990. He is a keynote speaker who has spoken at major training and human resources conferences. He co-wrote Crucial Conversations and other bestsellers on how people change behavior.
