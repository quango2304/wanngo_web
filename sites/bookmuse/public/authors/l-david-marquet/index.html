L. David Marquet

LD

Retired US Navy submarine captain and leadership author

L. David Marquet graduated from the US Naval Academy in 1981 and served in the Navy's submarine force. He commanded the nuclear-powered attack submarine USS Santa Fe from 1999 to 2001. Its crew was at the bottom of the fleet when he arrived, and the ship improved sharply under him. After leaving the Navy he became a leadership consultant. Turn the Ship Around! tells the Santa Fe story, and he later wrote Leadership Is Language.
