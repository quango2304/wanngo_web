Kristen Willeumier

KW

American neuroscientist and author

Kristen Willeumier has a doctorate in neurobiology from UCLA. She was research director at the Amen Clinics, a brain-imaging clinic, where she helped lead brain-scan studies of NFL players. She has worked for more than twenty years in brain research and has written about brain health for a wide audience. Biohack Your Brain is her book on everyday habits that help the brain. She lives in Los Angeles.
