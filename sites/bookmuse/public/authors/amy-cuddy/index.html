Amy Cuddy

American social psychologist and author · Born 1972

Amy Cuddy is an American social psychologist. She studied psychology at the University of Colorado Boulder and earned her PhD at Princeton, then taught at Rutgers, Northwestern's Kellogg School and Harvard Business School. Her research looks at how people judge each other and how body language relates to feelings of power. Her 2012 TED talk on body language was widely watched, and it led to her book Presence (2015).
