Ayn Rand

Russian-born American novelist and philosopher · 1905–1982

Ayn Rand was born Alisa Rosenbaum in Saint Petersburg, Russia. She studied history, moved to the United States in 1926 and worked in Hollywood as an extra and then a junior screenwriter. Her novel The Fountainhead (1943) made her famous, and Atlas Shrugged (1957) became an international bestseller. She called her ideas Objectivism: people should pursue their own happiness through productive work, with reason as their only absolute. She died in New York in 1982.
