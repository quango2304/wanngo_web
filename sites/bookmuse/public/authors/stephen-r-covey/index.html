Stephen R. Covey

American author, educator and leadership teacher · 1932–2012

Stephen R. Covey was an American author and teacher born in Salt Lake City, Utah. He earned an MBA from Harvard Business School and a doctorate from Brigham Young University, where he later taught business. He left teaching to train leaders and helped build the Covey Leadership Center, later part of FranklinCovey. His book The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People came out in 1989 and became one of the best-selling self-help books. He also wrote First Things First, The 8th Habit and The Leader in Me.
