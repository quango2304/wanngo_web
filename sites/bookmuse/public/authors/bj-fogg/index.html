BJ Fogg

Behavior scientist and founder of the Stanford Behavior Design Lab

BJ Fogg is a behavior scientist who founded the Behavior Design Lab at Stanford University. He teaches companies how human behavior really works and helped his students build apps used by millions of people. In 2011 he began teaching his Tiny Habits method to the public, and he later started the Tiny Habits Academy. His 2019 book Tiny Habits explains the method. He lives in Northern California and Maui.
