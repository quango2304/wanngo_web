Kamal Ravikant

KR

Author and Silicon Valley entrepreneur

Kamal Ravikant is an author and entrepreneur. He served as a US Army infantry soldier, meditated with monks in the Himalayas, and walked 550 miles across Spain on a pilgrimage. In Silicon Valley he cofounded several companies and a venture capital firm. After one of his companies failed, he went through a hard time and wrote a vow to love himself. That became his book Love Yourself Like Your Life Depends on It. He has also written Rebirth and Live Your Truth.
