Jessica Joelle Alexander

JJ

American author and cultural researcher on Danish parenting

Jessica Joelle Alexander is an American author, columnist and cultural researcher. She studied psychology and has taught communication and writing skills in Scandinavia and Central Europe. She is married to a Dane, and that led her to ask what Danes do differently with their children. She wrote The Danish Way of Parenting with Iben Dissing Sandahl, and later The Danish Way of Education. She also speaks about families and digital life.
