Michael Pollan

American writer and journalist on food and nature · Born 1955

Michael Pollan is an American writer and journalist who writes about food, farming and the natural world. He was born on Long Island, New York. He teaches science and environmental journalism at the University of California, Berkeley. His books include The Botany of Desire, The Omnivore's Dilemma, In Defense of Food and Cooked. The Omnivore's Dilemma follows four meals back to where their food began.
