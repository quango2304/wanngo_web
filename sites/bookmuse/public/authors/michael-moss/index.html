Michael Moss

American investigative journalist and author · Born 1955

Michael Moss is an American journalist who worked at The Wall Street Journal and then The New York Times. He won the 2010 Pulitzer Prize for Explanatory Reporting. His first book, Palace Coup (1989), is about Harry and Leona Helmsley. For Salt Sugar Fat (2013), he spent three and a half years interviewing people inside the food industry and reading its internal records. He also wrote Hooked (2021), about food companies and our addictions.
