Catherine Price

CP

American science journalist and author

Catherine Price is an award-winning health and science journalist. Her writing has appeared in publications such as the New York Times. Her books include Vitamania, How to Break Up with Your Phone, The Power of Fun and The Amazing Generation, which she wrote with Jonathan Haidt. She also founded Screen/Life Balance, which helps people build a healthier relationship with their devices. She lives in Philadelphia.
