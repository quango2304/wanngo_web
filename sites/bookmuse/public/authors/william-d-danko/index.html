William D. Danko

WD

American marketing professor and co-author on wealth

William D. Danko earned his bachelor's degree and MBA at the University at Albany and a PhD from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in 1983. He joined the Albany School of Business that year and spent 31 years on its marketing faculty, nine of them as department chair. As a student he worked on Thomas Stanley's first study of wealthy Americans, and Stanley became his research partner. Together they wrote The Millionaire Next Door. He later co-wrote Richer than a Millionaire.
