Philippa Perry

British psychotherapist, writer and broadcaster · Born 1957

Philippa Perry is a British psychotherapist, writer and broadcaster. She wrote the graphic book Couch Fiction in 2010 and How to Stay Sane in 2012. She has presented radio and television programs about the mind, including one on children who lie. The Book You Wish Your Parents Had Read came out in 2019 and grew from her work with parents and her own family. She followed it with The Book You Want Everyone You Love To Read in 2023.
