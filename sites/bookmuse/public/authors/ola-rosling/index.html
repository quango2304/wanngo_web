Ola Rosling

Swedish statistician and co-founder of Gapminder · Born 1975

Ola Rosling is a Swedish statistician and the son of Hans Rosling. With his father and his wife, Anna Rosling Rönnlund, he co-founded the Gapminder Foundation. He led the making of Trendalyzer, software that draws statistics as moving bubble charts, and Google bought it in 2007. He then worked at Google on public data. After his father died he finished Factfulness with Anna.
