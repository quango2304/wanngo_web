Baek Sehee

BS

Korean writer and publishing worker · 1990–2025

Baek Sehee was a Korean writer, born in 1990. She studied creative writing at university and worked at a publishing house. For ten years she was treated for dysthymia, a steady, light depression. She turned her therapy sessions into the essays and books that began with I Want to Die but I Want to Eat Tteokbokki, which has since been published in seven Asian languages and in English. She died in 2025, at 35.
