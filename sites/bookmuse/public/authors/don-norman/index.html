Don Norman

Cognitive scientist and design researcher · Born 1935

Don Norman is an American researcher, professor and writer. He trained as an engineer and then became a cognitive psychologist. He taught at the University of California, San Diego, where he founded its Design Lab, and he worked at Apple and HP. He co-founded the Nielsen Norman Group with Jakob Nielsen. His book The Design of Everyday Things first came out in 1988 as The Psychology of Everyday Things.
