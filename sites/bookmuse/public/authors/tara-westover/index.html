Tara Westover

American historian and memoir writer · Born 1986

Tara Westover was born in Idaho in 1986 and grew up in the mountains there. She studied history at Brigham Young University and finished in 2008. A Gates Cambridge scholarship took her to the University of Cambridge, where she earned a master's degree and then a PhD in intellectual history in 2014. She also spent time as a visiting fellow at Harvard. Her memoir Educated came out in 2018, and Time named her one of the 100 most influential people of 2019.
