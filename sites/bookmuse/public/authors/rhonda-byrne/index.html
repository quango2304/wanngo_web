Rhonda Byrne

RB

Australian television writer and producer · Born 1951

Rhonda Byrne is an Australian television writer and producer. She made the 2006 film The Secret, then wrote the book of the same name the same year. Both present the law of attraction, the belief that your thoughts shape what happens to you. The idea is a belief and not a proven science. She later wrote sequels, including The Power and The Magic.
