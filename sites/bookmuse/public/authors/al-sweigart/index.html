Al Sweigart

Software developer and author of beginner Python books

Al Sweigart is a software developer, author and artist. He is a Fellow of the Python Software Foundation. He wrote Automate the Boring Stuff with Python for people with no programming experience. He also made open source Python modules, including PyAutoGUI and pyperclip, which the book uses. He puts his other books on his website for free under a Creative Commons license.
