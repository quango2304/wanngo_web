Tahl Raz

TR

Journalist and business book co-writer

Tahl Raz is a journalist who writes about ideas and stories that help people and companies grow. He has written for magazines such as Inc. He co-wrote the bestseller Never Eat Alone with Keith Ferrazzi in 2005. He then worked with Chris Voss on Never Split the Difference, published in 2016. He also coaches executives and speaks about the changing world of work.
