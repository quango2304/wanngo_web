Benjamin Graham

Investor, teacher and writer, the father of value investing · 1894–1976

Benjamin Graham was born in London and grew up in New York. His family lost its money when he was a boy, and he later won a scholarship to Columbia University. He went to Wall Street, ran his own investment firm, and taught investing at Columbia. His book Security Analysis, written with David Dodd, turned stock picking into a profession. The Intelligent Investor, first published in 1949, brought his ideas to ordinary savers. Warren Buffett was his student and later his employee.
