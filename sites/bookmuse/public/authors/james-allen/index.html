James Allen

British writer of short books on thought and character · 1864–1912

James Allen was born in Leicester, England, into a working-class family. He left school at fifteen after his father died, and later worked as a private secretary and then in journalism. In 1898 he began writing for a magazine called The Herald of the Golden Age, and his first book, From Poverty to Power, came out in 1901. His small book As a Man Thinketh became his most famous, and is now read around the world. He lived in the seaside town of Ilfracombe in his last years and died of tuberculosis in 1912.
