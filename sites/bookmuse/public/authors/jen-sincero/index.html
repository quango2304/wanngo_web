Jen Sincero

American author, speaker and success coach · Born 1965

Jen Sincero is an American author, speaker and success coach. She worked in the music business and wrote two earlier books before she turned to self-help. You Are a Badass (2013) became a bestseller and started a series of books. You Are a Badass at Making Money (2017) is about the beliefs that hold people back from earning more. She also wrote You Are a Badass Every Day and Badass Habits.
