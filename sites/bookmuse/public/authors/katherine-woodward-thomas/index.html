Katherine Woodward Thomas

KW

Therapist, coach and author of Calling in “The One”

Katherine Woodward Thomas was a licensed marriage and family therapist who became a relationship teacher and coach. Her first book, Calling in “The One,” grew out of her own search for a partner. She later wrote Conscious Uncoupling, a book about ending a marriage with kindness, and she is credited with coining that phrase. She trained coaches in both methods. Her website says she died in April 2026.
