Joseph Murphy

JM

Irish-born American minister and writer on the mind · 1898–1981

Joseph Murphy was born in Ballydehob, County Cork, Ireland. He moved to the United States in 1922 and worked as a pharmacist before turning to the ministry. In Los Angeles he became a minister in the Divine Science church and gave public lectures and radio talks on the power of the mind. He wrote more than thirty books. The Power of Your Subconscious Mind, first published in 1963, was the best known and became a bestseller.
