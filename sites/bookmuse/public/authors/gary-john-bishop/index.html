Gary John Bishop

GJ

Scottish-born coach and author of personal development books

Gary John Bishop was born and raised in Glasgow, Scotland, and moved to the United States in 1997. He trained for years in personal development and became a senior program director with a large personal development company. He has run programs for thousands of people around the world and calls his style urban philosophy. He lives in Florida with his wife and three sons. His book Unf*ck Yourself was first published in 2016 and reissued by HarperOne in 2017.
