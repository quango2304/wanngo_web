Bookmuse for Kobo

Your library, reading place, highlights and words on your Kobo e-reader, in sync with the Bookmuse app on your phone.

What you get

  • Your library and reading place, synced with the phone app
  • Highlights, notes and bookmarks
  • Tap a word for an offline dictionary, and save it to your words
  • Word review with flashcards, and reading time and stats
  • Books from USB, catalogs and Dropbox

There is no listening and no AI on the Kobo. An account is optional: without one, Bookmuse reads the books you copy over USB.

Install

  1. Connect the Kobo to a computer with a USB cable. Tap Connect on the Kobo if it asks.
  2. Copy KoboRoot.tgz into the hidden .kobo folder on the Kobo. To see hidden folders, press Cmd+Shift+. in the Mac Finder, or choose View › Show › Hidden items in Windows.
  3. Eject the Kobo and unplug the cable. It restarts and installs by itself, which takes a few minutes. Keep it on. (With the cable still in, Bookmuse waits for the next restart to open.)
  4. Bookmuse opens by itself. When the Kobo starts again, it shows its own home screen for a few seconds, then opens Bookmuse. It does this every time the Kobo starts.
  5. Sign in by scanning the QR code with the Bookmuse app on your phone, or with your email and password. You can skip this and read books you copy over USB.

Wi-Fi: Bookmuse asks for your Wi-Fi network the first time it opens; you can change it later in Settings › Wi-Fi.

Opening Bookmuse

The Kobo opens Bookmuse every time it starts. Quit to Kobo in Bookmuse Settings takes you to the Kobo's own screens until the next restart.

If Bookmuse doesn't open by itself, open it from the menu: tap the menu button at the bottom right of the Kobo's home screen, then tap Bookmuse. You need this after Quit to Kobo, when the startup switch is off, and after a Kobo firmware update. (The menu entry comes from NickelMenu, which is in the same file.)

To stop it opening at startup, turn off Settings › Open Bookmuse at Startup in Bookmuse. From a computer you can also create an empty file named no-autostart in .adds/bookmuse; delete it to turn the setting back on. Bookmuse also stays closed when the Kobo starts with a USB cable connected to a computer, and when it was closed within a minute at the last two starts. Turn the switch off and on again to reset that.

Updating

In Bookmuse, open Settings › About › Check for Update. The new version downloads over Wi-Fi and installs when the Kobo restarts.

After a Kobo firmware update

A firmware update from Kobo removes the startup hook and can remove NickelMenu, and the Bookmuse entry with it. Until you copy KoboRoot.tgz again (steps 1 to 3), Bookmuse won't open by itself; if its menu entry is still there, open it from the menu. Your books and settings stay on the Kobo.

Uninstall

  1. Connect the Kobo by USB. Your computer may hide folders that start with a dot; see step 2 above.
  2. Delete the .adds/bookmuse folder and the .adds/nm/bookmuse file (and .kobo/KoboRoot.tgz if an update is waiting). Bookmuse stops opening at startup as soon as the folder is gone.
  3. To remove NickelMenu too, create an empty file named uninstall in .adds/nm.
  4. Eject the Kobo and let it restart. Your books and your Bookmuse account are not touched.
