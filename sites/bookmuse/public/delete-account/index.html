Delete Your Bookmuse Account

Bookmuse, by Quan Ngo · Last updated November 5, 2026

In short: in the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete Account. Your account and everything in it are deleted right away and can't be restored.

In the app

  1. Open Bookmuse on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android device and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings › Account › Delete Account.
  3. Sign in with Apple or Google once more, or enter your password, to confirm.

Your other devices remove the account's library the next time they open Bookmuse. The web dashboard can't delete an account.

By email

If you can't use the app, email admin@bookmuse.wanngo.com with the subject "Delete my Bookmuse account", from your account's address, or tell us which address it is. For Apple's Hide My Email, include the relay address (Apple Account settings › Sign in with Apple › Bookmuse). We confirm by replying to that address, then delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-ins, profile and every connected device, dashboard and AI agent.
  • Your library, including items you deleted earlier, your book files and covers, and your AI usage counts and reported answers.
  • Your share links, and your feed posts, comments, likes, follows, blocks and reports, including your club posts and memberships. A club you own passes to an admin or the longest-standing member, or is deleted if you are its only member.
  • Your Dropbox and Google Drive connections kept in your account. We revoke that access, and Sign in with Apple too. With the Drive browser, Google Drive is kept on your devices only; you can also remove Bookmuse in your Google Account's permissions.

The database is cleared at once and stored files within minutes. We keep no backups.

What stays

  • A stored book file that another user also added stays for them, no longer linked to you. A copy someone added through your share link stays theirs.
  • Short technical logs (no content) until they rotate out soon after. A record of the cleanup task stays for 24 hours.
  • Public covers and pictures may stay briefly in Cloudflare's and browsers' caches, with no link to your account.
  • Your files in your own Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud, and what an AI agent already read, are outside Bookmuse.

Keep the account, delete some data

Erase All Data (Settings) clears your library from your account and all your devices. It keeps the account, profile, feed posts, reported answers and Drive or Dropbox connections. You can also delete single items, and disconnect services in Settings › Connections. Without an account, deleting the app removes everything. More in the privacy policy.
