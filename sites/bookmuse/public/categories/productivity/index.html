Productivity book summaries

36 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. Thinking, Fast and SlowDaniel Kahneman · 18 minYour mind runs on a fast, automatic storyteller that feels sure and a slow, lazy checker that mostly signs off, so thinking better means learning the storyteller's tricks by name and slowing down when the stakes are high.
  2. Atomic HabitsJames Clear · 17 minSmall habits, repeated every day, build the person you want to become. Make good habits obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying, and flip those four laws to break bad ones.
  3. The ONE ThingGary Keller, Jay Papasan · 16 minBig results come from going small: find the ONE Thing that makes everything else easier or unnecessary, and do it first.
  4. Ego Is the EnemyRyan Holiday · 19 minEgo is an unhealthy belief in your own importance, and it works against you when you aim for a goal, when you reach it and when you fail. Humility and steady work are the cure.
  5. Getting Things DoneDavid Allen · 20 minYour mind is "for having ideas, not for holding them", so get everything that has your attention out of your head, decide the next step on each thing, and review it all every week.
  6. QuietSusan Cain · 19 minOur culture treats the bold, talkative style as the best one. Yet up to half of Americans are introverts, and quiet strengths are powers we cannot afford to waste.
  7. Outwitting the DevilNapoleon Hill · 18 minIn this book the Devil is fear living in the unused part of your mind, and you outwit him by deciding what you want and thinking for yourself.
  8. The Personal MBAJosh Kaufman · 22 minEvery business is five simple parts, and you can learn them yourself, along with how people and systems behave, without an expensive degree.
  9. The Power of HabitCharles Duhigg · 20 minHabits are loops that run without thought, and once you know how a loop works, you can change it.
  10. How to Read a BookMortimer J. Adler · 20 minA hard book can lift your understanding, but only if you work on it: first to see its shape, then to grasp what it says, and last to judge it fairly.
  11. Eat That Frog!Brian Tracy · 19 minStart each day with your biggest, most important task, and stay on it until it is done.
  12. Team of TeamsStanley McChrystal, Tantum Collins, David Silverman and Chris Fussell · 20 minA large organization built to be efficient will lose in a fast, tangled world, unless it learns to work as a team of teams: everyone sees the whole picture, and the people closest to a problem decide.
  13. On WritingStephen King · 18 minWriting is one mind reaching another, and hard work can make a competent writer good: read a lot, write a lot, use plain honest words, find the story, and cut your second draft.
  14. Dopamine DetoxThibaut Meurisse · 15 minConstant stimulation makes your important work feel dull. Lower the noise, and hard things get easier to start.
  15. The 5 AM ClubRobin Sharma · 14 minGet up at 5 AM and spend the first hour on yourself in three parts, move, reflect and grow, and keep it up for 66 days until it is automatic, while you guard your focus and your rest.
  16. The 7 Habits of Highly Effective PeopleStephen R. Covey · 19 minLasting change works from the inside out: change how you see things, then build seven habits that take you from depending on others, to standing on your own, to working well with others.
  17. What the Most Successful People Do Before BreakfastLaura Vanderkam · 17 minThe most successful people spend their early mornings, their weekends and their work hours on purpose, so what matters most gets done first.
  18. The 80/20 PrincipleRichard Koch · 17 minA few causes make most of the results. Find your few, give them more, and give the rest less, at work and in life.
  19. Deep WorkCal Newport · 20 minThe power to focus without distraction on hard work is getting rarer and worth more every year, so people who build their work life around it will thrive.
  20. No ExcusesBrian Tracy · 17 minSelf-discipline, doing the right thing at the right time even when you do not feel like it, is the one skill that makes every other success rule work, and anyone can build it as a habit.
  21. Emotional IntelligenceDaniel Goleman · 19 minFeelings can swamp your thinking, or they can guide it. Goleman says five skills decide which one happens, and that these skills can matter as much as IQ, and sometimes more.
  22. Don’t Overthink ItAnne Bogel · 17 minOverthinking eats the hours of your life. You can win them back with small shifts: aim for good enough, decide once, and say yes to what you love.
  23. The Science of Self-DisciplinePeter Hollins · 18 minSelf-discipline is a skill you can train, and it works best when you also shape your surroundings, your people and your habits so that you need less willpower.
  24. Lives of the StoicsRyan Holiday, Stephen Hanselman · 18 minThe Stoics were judged by what they did, not what they said, and their lives and deaths show how to live well: with four virtues, a calm focus on what you control, and daily practice.
  25. Million Dollar HabitsStellan Moreira · 16 minYour life follows your habits, so build the habits of a calm, happy mind first, then the habits that grow your money.
  26. Tiny HabitsBJ Fogg · 19 minBJ Fogg says that failing to change is a design flaw, not a flaw in you: pick a tiny behavior you already want, tie it to something you already do, and celebrate it right away.
  27. The Miracle MorningHal Elrod · 17 minYour success rises only as far as you do, so give the first hour of each day to six simple practices that build the person you want to be.
  28. Digital MinimalismCal Newport · 19 minUse technology only for the few things you value most, and be happy to miss out on the rest.
  29. How to Break Up with Your PhoneCatherine Price · 19 minPhones are built to hook your attention, so willpower alone is not enough. A 30-day plan can let you keep what you love about your phone and decide for yourself what gets your attention.
  30. ScrumJeff Sutherland · 18 minA plan made in advance is only a guess, so Scrum works in short cycles, ends each one with finished work, and fixes whatever slows the team down.
  31. Speed ReadingKam Knight · 18 minReading faster is a matter of changing a few habits: prepare first, take in groups of words, keep the inner voice quiet and never go back, then make sure you remember.
  32. Stolen FocusJohann Hari · 17 minHari argues that our focus is falling apart because of twelve forces in the way we live, so fixing it takes personal steps and changes we make together.
  33. The 4-Hour WorkweekTimothy Ferriss · 19 minYou do not need a million dollars or a retirement party to live freely: free your time and your place first, in four steps called DEAL.
  34. The 5 Second RuleMel Robbins · 18 minWhen you feel an urge to act on a goal, count 5-4-3-2-1 and move before your brain stops you: change is made of these five-second acts of everyday courage.
  35. The Pomodoro TechniqueFrancesco Cirillo · 16 minWork in 25-minute boxes with real breaks, and time stops being an enemy and becomes a tool you can measure and improve.
  36. Unlimited MemoryKevin Horsley · 15 minA good memory is a skill, not a gift: pay full attention, turn what you want to keep into a silly moving picture, hang it on a place you already know, and review it.
