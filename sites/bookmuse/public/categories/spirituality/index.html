Spirituality book summaries

14 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. Being MortalAtul Gawande · 20 minWhen a body can no longer be fixed, safety and a longer life are not enough: people want to stay the authors of their own story, so doctors, care homes and families must ask what matters most and shape care around it.
  2. The Four AgreementsDon Miguel Ruiz · 16 minAs children we agreed to beliefs we never chose, and four new agreements can replace the ones that make us suffer.
  3. Breaking the Habit of Being YourselfJoe Dispenza · 19 minTo change your life, you must stop being the self you have memorized, then rehearse a new self until it feels real.
  4. Becoming SupernaturalJoe Dispenza · 23 minYour thoughts, actions and feelings keep your future a copy of your past, and you can change them ahead of time with a clear intention and an elevated emotion, until your body believes the new life has begun.
  5. The SecretRhonda Byrne · 17 minYour thoughts and feelings send out a signal, and the law of attraction brings back whatever matches it.
  6. Don’t Believe Everything You ThinkJoseph Nguyen · 17 minJoseph Nguyen says that events do not make you suffer, your thinking about them does, and when that thinking settles, peace is what is left.
  7. The Untethered SoulMichael A. Singer · 18 minYou are not your thoughts or your feelings. You are the one who notices them, and you grow free by letting them pass instead of guarding yourself.
  8. Think Like a MonkJay Shetty · 19 minYou do not need robes or a monastery to find peace and purpose: you train your mind the way monks do, by letting go, growing and giving.
  9. Tuesdays with MorrieMitch Albom · 18 minFacing death honestly shows what matters in life: love, giving, attention and forgiveness, not money, status or hurry.
  10. Women Who Run With the WolvesClarissa Pinkola Estés · 23 minEvery woman has a wild, instinctive self that knows what is true and what she needs. Being trained to be tame buries it, and old stories show how to dig it up and live with it.
  11. The Power of Positive ThinkingNorman Vincent Peale · 17 minWhat you keep in your mind shapes how you feel and act, so push out fear and doubt, fill your mind with faith, and expect the best.
  12. The Bhagavad GitaVyasa · 17 minYou are a deathless self, so do your duty with a steady mind, let go of the reward, and make God your refuge.
  13. Love Yourself Like Your Life Depends on ItKamal Ravikant · 14 minMake a vow to love yourself, then repeat “I love myself” every day with feeling, until your thoughts, and then your life, follow.
  14. MeditationsMarcus Aurelius · 16 minYou cannot control what happens, only what you think and do, so judge calmly, treat people kindly and do your work now.
