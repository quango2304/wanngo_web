Business & Career book summaries

33 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. Crucial ConversationsJoseph Grenny, Kerry Patterson, Ron McMillan, Al Switzler, Emily Gregory · 20 minMost problems last because we dodge or botch the talks that matter most, and the way out is to make it safe for everyone to say what they think, starting with your own motives and your own stories.
  2. Surrounded by IdiotsThomas Erikson · 17 minIn Erikson's model, people act in four main styles, and you get through to anyone by meeting them on their terms, not yours.
  3. The Magic of Thinking BigDavid J. Schwartz · 18 minHow far you go depends on how big you think: believe you can, drop your excuses and act, and your results grow to match.
  4. Think AgainAdam Grant · 20 minBeing smart is not enough: in a world that changes fast, rethinking and unlearning matter just as much, and you build them by thinking like a scientist.
  5. Zero to OnePeter Thiel, Blake Masters · 20 minCopying what works takes the world from 1 to n; real progress is making something new, from 0 to 1.
  6. Shoe DogPhil Knight · 18 minPhil Knight built Nike from a crazy idea. He did it by believing in it, by giving odd and driven people room to run with it, and by never stopping when money or partners let him down.
  7. Playing to WinA.G. Lafley and Roger L. Martin · 19 minStrategy is not a vision or a plan. It is a set of five connected choices about how you will win, and you can test those choices by asking “What would have to be true?”
  8. Steve JobsWalter Isaacson · 23 minSteve Jobs made products people loved by joining art with technology, controlling the whole experience and keeping only what mattered, and the same intensity that drove the work often hurt the people around him.
  9. The Personal MBAJosh Kaufman · 22 minEvery business is five simple parts, and you can learn them yourself, along with how people and systems behave, without an expensive degree.
  10. Who Moved My Cheese?Spencer Johnson · 16 minChange always comes, and the fear in your own head holds you back more than the change does, so notice it early, let go of the old and move toward something better.
  11. Atlas ShruggedAyn Rand · 18 minThe world runs on the minds of people who think and build. Punish them for it, and they will leave, and everything stops with them.
  12. Automate the Boring Stuff with PythonAl Sweigart · 20 minA short program can do in seconds the repetitive computer jobs that eat your hours, and you do not have to be a software engineer to write one.
  13. The Lean StartupEric Ries · 19 minA startup exists to learn how to build a business that lasts, and it learns fastest by testing each guess with real customers in small steps.
  14. Turn the Ship Around!L. David Marquet · 20 minIn Marquet's account, people treated as followers act like followers; give them control, plus the competence and the clarity to use it, and they act like leaders.
  15. Elon MuskAshlee Vance · 19 minElon Musk builds companies to solve problems he has cared about since he was young, and he gets there by learning fast, risking his own money and asking for the impossible.
  16. Invent & WanderJeff Bezos and Walter Isaacson · 17 minBezos built Amazon by thinking in years, serving customers instead of rivals, and inventing through many bold bets, and he tried to keep it Day One as the company grew.
  17. How To Make Sh*t HappenSean Whalen · 15 minEverything you do in a day fits four areas: your body, the people in your life, your mind and your work. Give each one a planned action every day, and the chaos turns into results.
  18. How to Win Friends and Influence People in the Digital AgeDale Carnegie & Associates · 20 minCarnegie's old rules still work online: you win friends and influence people by leaving each one a little better, and that starts with real interest in them.
  19. No ExcusesBrian Tracy · 17 minSelf-discipline, doing the right thing at the right time even when you do not feel like it, is the one skill that makes every other success rule work, and anyone can build it as a habit.
  20. The Psychology of MoneyMorgan Housel · 20 minHow you behave with money matters much more than how much you know about it.
  21. Million Dollar HabitsStellan Moreira · 16 minYour life follows your habits, so build the habits of a calm, happy mind first, then the habits that grow your money.
  22. PrinciplesRay Dalio · 21 minYou get what you want by facing what is true, learning from your mistakes, and deciding together with the smartest people who disagree with you.
  23. Talk Like TEDCarmine Gallo · 18 minThe talks people love reach the heart first, then give a fresh idea in a way people can see, feel and remember, in the speaker's own voice.
  24. Pre-SuasionRobert Cialdini · 19 minBefore you ask for a yes, steer what the other person is thinking about. That moment can matter more than the ask itself.
  25. The Diary of a CEOSteven Bartlett · 18 minGreat things are built by people who follow a few lasting laws about themselves, their story, their thinking and their team.
  26. The 10X RuleGrant Cardone · 17 minSet your goals ten times higher than feels reasonable, then take ten times the action you think they need.
  27. $100M OffersAlex Hormozi · 20 minPeople pay for value, not for cost: build an offer worth far more than its price, and buyers stop comparing and start saying yes.
  28. CuesVanessa Van Edwards · 18 minPeople judge you on warmth and competence, and they read both from tiny signals called cues. You can learn to read them and to send them on purpose.
  29. Leaders Eat LastSimon Sinek · 17 minPeople give their best when they feel safe with each other, and a leader's job is to build that safety, even when it costs the leader something.
  30. The 2-Hour Job SearchSteve Dalton · 17 minOnline applications pile up unread, so jobs go to people who have someone inside speaking for them.
  31. The 4-Hour WorkweekTimothy Ferriss · 19 minYou do not need a million dollars or a retirement party to live freely: free your time and your place first, in four steps called DEAL.
  32. The 80/20 CEOBill Canady · 18 minA business gets stronger when its leaders find the small share of customers and products that bring most of the profit, put their effort there, and run a simple hundred-day system to get started.
  33. The Great CEO WithinMatt Mochary · 17 minA great CEO is trained, not born: fix your own habits first, then run a simple written system that keeps a growing team pointed the same way.
