Happiness book summaries

35 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. The Mountain Is YouBrianna Wiest · 17 minSelf-sabotage is not a flaw. It is a way of coping that meets a need you cannot see, so you get free by finding the need, feeling what you avoid, and changing one small step at a time.
  2. The Monk Who Sold His FerrariRobin Sharma · 18 minA fulfilling life is built from the inside: a trained mind, a clear purpose, daily self-improvement and a life of giving, lived now and not later.
  3. IkigaiHéctor García, Francesc Miralles · 18 minA long, happy life starts with a reason to get up each morning, kept alive by staying busy, close to friends, light on food, gentle in movement and calm in pace.
  4. The Body Keeps the ScoreBessel van der Kolk · 21 minTrauma is not only what happened. It is the mark left on the brain and body, and healing means helping them feel safe in the present.
  5. The Courage to Be DislikedIchiro Kishimi, Fumitake Koga · 18 minPeople are not stuck because of their past. They are stuck because change feels scary, and the way out is the courage to live your own life, even if some people dislike you.
  6. Good Vibes, Good LifeVex King · 18 minVex King argues that loving yourself, which means accepting who you are while you keep growing, lifts your mood, and that your mood decides what comes back to you.
  7. Man’s Search for MeaningViktor E. Frankl · 16 minPeople are driven most by a search for meaning, not by pleasure or power, and a person with a reason to live can bear almost any hardship, because one freedom can never be taken away: the freedom to choose how to respond.
  8. The Obstacle Is the WayRyan Holiday · 19 minWhatever blocks your way can be used. See it clearly, act on it with energy, and accept what you cannot change, and the obstacle becomes the way forward.
  9. The Power of Positive ThinkingNorman Vincent Peale · 17 minWhat you keep in your mind shapes how you feel and act, so push out fear and doubt, fill your mind with faith, and expect the best.
  10. The SecretRhonda Byrne · 17 minYour thoughts and feelings send out a signal, and the law of attraction brings back whatever matches it.
  11. 101 Essays That Will Change The Way You ThinkBrianna Wiest · 18 minYour problem is rarely what happened. It is how you think about it, so a changed mind can change a life.
  12. 12 Rules for LifeJordan B. Peterson · 20 minLife is hard and full of chaos, and the best answer is to take responsibility for yourself: stand tall, care for yourself, tell the truth, and aim at something worth the cost.
  13. As a Man ThinkethJames Allen · 15 minYour thoughts grow into your character, your circumstances and your health, so the way to change your life is to change what you think.
  14. Psycho-CyberneticsMaxwell Maltz, MD · 19 minYour mind steers you toward the picture you hold of yourself. Change that picture, with vivid imagination, calm practice and new habits, and you change what you do.
  15. DareBarry McDonagh · 19 minAnxiety is a wave of nervous energy, and it traps you only when you fear it, so you heal by moving with the wave, not against it.
  16. Hinch Yourself HappyMrs. Hinch · 18 minA home you love and look after can calm a worried mind, because cleaning gives your thoughts a job, a finish line and a small win.
  17. I Want to Die but I Want to Eat TteokbokkiBaek Sehee · 17 minFeeling low and wanting a good meal can live in one person, and a mind that thinks in extremes makes the hurt worse; a middle ground, taken one small step at a time, helps.
  18. The Good LifeRobert Waldinger and Marc Schulz · 20 minGood relationships keep us happier, healthier and alive longer, and it is never too late to build them.
  19. Emotional BlackmailSusan Forward with Donna Frazier · 18 minEmotional blackmail is a threat from someone close: do what I want, or you will suffer. It works through fear, obligation and guilt, and it ends when you change how you respond.
  20. The Art of StillnessPico Iyer · 17 minThe world keeps speeding up, so one of the most useful things you can do is go nowhere on purpose: sit still often, and bring the calm back into your life.
  21. Unf*ck YourselfGary John Bishop · 19 minWhat you tell yourself shapes your life. So say seven strong sentences in the present, and then act, whatever you feel.
  22. The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ckMark Manson · 17 minA good life does not come from caring about more and feeling good all the time. It comes from choosing the few things worth caring about, and accepting the pain that comes with them.
  23. The Power of NowEckhart Tolle · 19 minYou are not your mind. The endless thinking in your head, and the old pain it feeds on, cause most of your suffering, and you end it by watching them and giving this moment your full attention.
  24. Don’t Overthink ItAnne Bogel · 17 minOverthinking eats the hours of your life. You can win them back with small shifts: aim for good enough, decide once, and say yes to what you love.
  25. Lives of the StoicsRyan Holiday, Stephen Hanselman · 18 minThe Stoics were judged by what they did, not what they said, and their lives and deaths show how to live well: with four virtues, a calm focus on what you control, and daily practice.
  26. The Code of the Extraordinary MindVishen Lakhiani · 19 minMany of the rules you live by were handed to you, not chosen. Question them, pick your own beliefs and habits, and be happy now while a bold vision pulls you forward.
  27. Declutter Your MindS. J. Scott, Barrie Davenport · 18 minYou cannot stop thoughts from coming, but you can learn to watch them, answer them, and clear what feeds them.
  28. Single On PurposeJohn Kim · 17 minYou cannot build a healthy bond with anyone until you build one with yourself, so use your single time, and your time inside a relationship, to know who you are.
  29. Stop Checking Your LikesSusie Moore · 17 minYou do not need anyone's approval to live the life you want, so stop waiting for it and give it to yourself.
  30. Dopamine NationAnna Lembke · 18 minPleasure and pain sit on one balance in your brain, so chasing pleasure and running from pain tip it toward pain, and you can reset it.
  31. Set Boundaries, Find PeaceNedra Glover Tawwab · 17 minA boundary is a need you say out loud and then back up with action, and the short discomfort of doing it is worth the peace you get.
  32. The Danish Way of ParentingJessica Joelle Alexander, Iben Dissing Sandahl · 18 minThe authors say Danes top the world's happiness lists because of how they raise their children, and that six tools, which spell PARENT, work in any family.
  33. The Let Them TheoryMel Robbins · 19 minYou cannot control other people, so say Let Them to let go of them, then say Let Me to choose what you do next.
  34. 13 Things Mentally Strong People Don't DoAmy Morin · 19 minYou are only as strong as your worst habits, so mental strength means dropping thirteen habits of thinking, feeling and acting that hold you back.
  35. How to Stop Worrying And Start LivingDale Carnegie · 17 minWorry wears down your body and clouds your mind, but you can break the habit by living one day at a time, facing the worst with clear facts, and choosing calm action.
