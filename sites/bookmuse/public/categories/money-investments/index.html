Money & Investments book summaries

13 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. The Intelligent InvestorBenjamin Graham, Jason Zweig · 19 minTreat a stock as a small piece of a real business: pay less than it is worth, plan for being wrong, and do not let the market’s moods or your own run your money.
  2. The Millionaire Next DoorThomas J. Stanley and William D. Danko · 17 minMost American millionaires are ordinary people who spend far less than they earn and invest the rest, while many high earners who look rich own little.
  3. The Simple Path to WealthJ.L. Collins · 18 minSpend less than you earn, avoid debt, and put the rest in one low-cost fund that owns the whole stock market. Then stay calm when it falls.
  4. The Next Millionaire Next DoorThomas J. Stanley and Sarah Stanley Fallaw · 19 minPeople build wealth with everyday habits: they spend less than they earn, ignore what others show off, and run their money like a job.
  5. The Science of Getting RichWallace D. Wattles · 20 minWallace D. Wattles claims that getting rich is an exact science: picture what you want, hold it with faith and thanks, and act well every day, and you will get rich.
  6. Capital in the 21st CenturyThomas Piketty · 21 minWhen wealth earns more each year than the economy grows, money from the past pulls ahead of pay from the present, and inequality rises unless people choose to act.
  7. Rich Dad Poor DadRobert Kiyosaki · 18 minWealth comes from financial education, not from a big paycheck: learn the difference between an asset and a liability, keep buying assets that put money in your pocket, and stay calm about the risk of losing.
  8. The Psychology of MoneyMorgan Housel · 20 minHow you behave with money matters much more than how much you know about it.
  9. Think and Grow Rich!Napoleon Hill · 17 minNapoleon Hill says every achievement starts as an idea. A burning goal, a written plan, quick decisions, steady effort and a few helpers turn it into real results.
  10. Money Master the GameTony Robbins · 18 minAnyone can build an income for life: decide how much of every paycheck you keep, avoid the hidden costs, and follow seven simple steps in order.
  11. You Are a Badass at Making MoneyJen Sincero · 17 minSincero argues that old beliefs about money hold your income down, so you change the beliefs, get clear on why you want more, and act on what scares you.
  12. I Will Teach You to Be RichRamit Sethi · 19 minYou get rich by getting a few big things right once, then letting automatic, low-cost investing do the rest.
  13. The Automatic MillionaireDavid Bach · 18 minYou can get rich on an ordinary income if you pay yourself first and make every money move automatic.
