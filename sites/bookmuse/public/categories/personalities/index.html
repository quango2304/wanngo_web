Personalities book summaries

13 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. The PrinceNiccolò Machiavelli · 16 minA ruler keeps power by judging people as they really are, relying on his own strength, never making his people hate him, and preparing in calm times for the day luck turns.
  2. MasteryRobert Greene · 18 minMastery is not a gift some people are born with. It grows in anyone who finds the work that fits them, then spends years in three phases of learning, until they can feel their whole field at once.
  3. Shoe DogPhil Knight · 18 minPhil Knight built Nike from a crazy idea. He did it by believing in it, by giving odd and driven people room to run with it, and by never stopping when money or partners let him down.
  4. I Am MalalaMalala Yousafzai, Christina Lamb · 17 minA girl who loved school kept speaking up for it when the Taliban banned it. They shot her for it, and her voice only grew louder.
  5. Born a CrimeTrevor Noah · 20 minApartheid made Trevor Noah’s birth a crime and tried to keep people apart, and a mother who loved hard, taught him to think and found the cracks in unjust rules gave him ways to cross every line.
  6. EducatedTara Westover · 18 minBeing educated means learning to weigh many voices and build your own mind, and for Tara Westover that cost her family.
  7. I’m Glad My Mom DiedJennette McCurdy · 19 minA girl raised to live for her mother’s approval loses track of what she wants, and she begins to heal only when she tells the truth about her mother and learns what she wants herself.
  8. Elon MuskAshlee Vance · 19 minElon Musk builds companies to solve problems he has cared about since he was young, and he gets there by learning fast, risking his own money and asking for the impossible.
  9. On WritingStephen King · 18 minWriting is one mind reaching another, and hard work can make a competent writer good: read a lot, write a lot, use plain honest words, find the story, and cut your second draft.
  10. The Immortal Life of Henrietta LacksRebecca Skloot · 20 minIn 1951 doctors took cells from a poor Black woman without telling her, and those cells became one of the most useful tools in medicine, while her family was never told.
  11. Surely You’re Joking, Mr. Feynman!Richard P. Feynman · 21 minReal understanding is something you test for yourself, with puzzles, plain examples, play and honesty, never with names, titles or the word of experts.
  12. The Laws of Human NatureRobert Greene · 22 minDeep forces such as emotion, self-love, envy and fear drive us without our knowing, and once you see them in others and in yourself, you stop being caught off guard.
  13. Steve JobsWalter Isaacson · 23 minSteve Jobs made products people loved by joining art with technology, controlling the whole experience and keeping only what mattered, and the same intensity that drove the work often hurt the people around him.
