Family book summaries

19 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. Born a CrimeTrevor Noah · 20 minApartheid made Trevor Noah’s birth a crime and tried to keep people apart, and a mother who loved hard, taught him to think and found the cracks in unjust rules gave him ways to cross every line.
  2. EducatedTara Westover · 18 minBeing educated means learning to weigh many voices and build your own mind, and for Tara Westover that cost her family.
  3. I’m Glad My Mom DiedJennette McCurdy · 19 minA girl raised to live for her mother’s approval loses track of what she wants, and she begins to heal only when she tells the truth about her mother and learns what she wants herself.
  4. When Breath Becomes AirPaul Kalanithi · 18 minDeath comes for everyone, so the real question is how to live with it: with other people, with honesty, and by going on.
  5. Why Does He Do That?Lundy Bancroft · 25 minAbuse comes from a man's attitudes of ownership and entitlement, not from his feelings, so a partner cannot fix him by understanding him or by being easier to live with.
  6. Hillbilly ElegyJ.D. Vance · 18 minA poor town's troubles are not only about lost jobs. They also come from chaos at home and a belief that your choices do not matter, and one steady, loving person can change a child's life.
  7. The Book You Wish Your Parents Had ReadPhilippa Perry · 18 minA child grows in the relationship with a parent, the way a plant grows in soil, so that relationship is the core of parenting.
  8. Drama FreeNedra Glover Tawwab · 18 minYou cannot change your family, but you can change how you take part in it, and that is enough to keep a hard relationship on your terms or to end it.
  9. Mindful Relationship HabitsBarrie Davenport, S.J. Scott · 17 minLove fades mostly from lack of attention, and a couple can bring it back by choosing small, mindful habits, one at a time.
  10. The 5 Love LanguagesGary Chapman · 16 minLove lasts when you learn which of five love languages your spouse understands best, and you choose to speak it.
  11. Hold Me TightSue Johnson · 19 minAdult love is a need for one safe person, so most fights are cries that ask are you there for me, and love lasts when partners learn to ask that clearly and answer with care.
  12. How to Talk So Kids Will Listen & Listen So Kids Will TalkAdele Faber, Elaine Mazlish · 18 minChildren listen, cooperate and grow when you accept their feelings and talk without blame, so their self-respect stays whole.
  13. The Whole-Brain ChildDaniel J. Siegel and Tina Payne Bryson · 18 minA hard moment with your child is a chance to help the parts of their brain work together.
  14. How to Stop Losing Your Sh*t with Your KidsCarla Naumburg · 18 minYou lose your temper when a tired, overloaded nervous system gets pushed, so take care of yourself first, and when your kids push anyway, notice, pause and do anything else.
  15. No-Drama DisciplineDaniel J. Siegel and Tina Payne Bryson · 17 minDiscipline means to teach. When a child misbehaves, connect first so the child can listen, then redirect toward a better choice.
  16. The 5 Love Languages of ChildrenGary Chapman, Ross Campbell · 16 minYour child needs to feel your love, not only to have it, and you can fill that need by speaking the love language she understands best.
  17. The 7 Habits of Highly Effective FamiliesStephen R. Covey · 19 minEvery family is off track most of the time; the strong ones know where they are going, change themselves first, and keep coming back.
  18. The Anxious GenerationJonathan Haidt · 17 minJonathan Haidt argues that children born after 1995 grew up anxious because adults overprotected them in the real world and underprotected them online.
  19. The Danish Way of ParentingJessica Joelle Alexander, Iben Dissing Sandahl · 18 minThe authors say Danes top the world's happiness lists because of how they raise their children, and that six tools, which spell PARENT, work in any family.
