Health book summaries

22 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. Being MortalAtul Gawande · 20 minWhen a body can no longer be fixed, safety and a longer life are not enough: people want to stay the authors of their own story, so doctors, care homes and families must ask what matters most and shape care around it.
  2. The Mind-Gut ConnectionEmeran Mayer · 20 minYour brain, your gut and the trillions of microbes in it talk to each other all day, and what you eat and how you feel changes what they say.
  3. Eat & RunScott Jurek, Steve Friedman · 16 minStrength to keep going is built by doing hard things one piece at a time, with good food, a calm mind and friends, and the journey matters more than the prize.
  4. I Want to Die but I Want to Eat TteokbokkiBaek Sehee · 17 minFeeling low and wanting a good meal can live in one person, and a mind that thinks in extremes makes the hurt worse; a middle ground, taken one small step at a time, helps.
  5. I’m Glad My Mom DiedJennette McCurdy · 19 minA girl raised to live for her mother’s approval loses track of what she wants, and she begins to heal only when she tells the truth about her mother and learns what she wants herself.
  6. Salt, Sugar and FatMichael Moss · 23 minFood companies build their products around salt, sugar and fat so that people want more, and they will not stop on their own.
  7. When Breath Becomes AirPaul Kalanithi · 18 minDeath comes for everyone, so the real question is how to live with it: with other people, with honesty, and by going on.
  8. The Immortal Life of Henrietta LacksRebecca Skloot · 20 minIn 1951 doctors took cells from a poor Black woman without telling her, and those cells became one of the most useful tools in medicine, while her family was never told.
  9. The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a HatOliver Sacks · 20 minA brain illness is never only something taken away: the person inside keeps working to stay themselves, so to understand a patient you need their story, not just a list of what is wrong.
  10. The Omnivore's DilemmaMichael Pollan · 19 minMost of what we eat comes from one cheap crop, corn, and its real cost is hidden. Knowing where your food comes from helps you choose better.
  11. Dopamine DetoxThibaut Meurisse · 15 minConstant stimulation makes your important work feel dull. Lower the noise, and hard things get easier to start.
  12. Why We SleepMatthew Walker · 18 minSleep is not one favor but many: each night it saves your memories, calms your feelings and protects your body, and Walker says we are losing too much of it.
  13. Drama FreeNedra Glover Tawwab · 18 minYou cannot change your family, but you can change how you take part in it, and that is enough to keep a hard relationship on your terms or to end it.
  14. My Age of AnxietyScott Stossel · 18 minAnxiety is fear of something that has not happened yet. It grows from body, mind, genes, childhood and stress, so no single cure covers it.
  15. Biohack Your BrainKristen Willeumier · 17 minAt any age, you can improve your brain with everyday habits: moving, eating well, drinking water, calming stress, thinking kindly and learning new things.
  16. Why Has Nobody Told Me This Before?Dr. Julie Smith · 18 minYour feelings are built from your thoughts, your body and your actions, so you have more power over them than you were taught, and a few simple tools put that power to use.
  17. Dopamine NationAnna Lembke · 18 minPleasure and pain sit on one balance in your brain, so chasing pleasure and running from pain tip it toward pain, and you can reset it.
  18. How to Be Your Own TherapistOwen O’Kane · 17 minYou can learn the skills a therapist teaches, and use them yourself for ten minutes a day to feel steadier.
  19. Reasons to Stay AliveMatt Haig · 17 minDepression is a real illness that lies about the future, and Matt Haig shows that small steps, other people and honest talk can lead back to life.
  20. Set Boundaries, Find PeaceNedra Glover Tawwab · 17 minA boundary is a need you say out loud and then back up with action, and the short discomfort of doing it is worth the peace you get.
  21. The 4-Hour BodyTimothy Ferriss · 18 minBig changes in your body come from the smallest steps that work, tracked with numbers and tested on yourself.
  22. The Anxious GenerationJonathan Haidt · 17 minJonathan Haidt argues that children born after 1995 grew up anxious because adults overprotected them in the real world and underprotected them online.
