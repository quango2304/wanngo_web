Negotiation book summaries

29 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. Crucial ConversationsJoseph Grenny, Kerry Patterson, Ron McMillan, Al Switzler, Emily Gregory · 20 minMost problems last because we dodge or botch the talks that matter most, and the way out is to make it safe for everyone to say what they think, starting with your own motives and your own stories.
  2. Nonviolent CommunicationMarshall B. Rosenberg · 18 minJudging and demanding make people defend themselves or give in out of fear. Saying what you see, feel, need and ask for, and hearing the same four things in others, lets people give from the heart.
  3. Getting to YesRoger Fisher, William Ury, Bruce Patton · 19 minYou do not have to choose between being hard and being soft. Settle the problem itself, not a contest of will, protect the person across the table, and look for a result both sides can call fair.
  4. Men Are from Mars, Women Are from VenusJohn Gray · 19 minMost clashes between a man and a woman come from forgetting they are different, and love lasts when each learns what the other needs.
  5. Surrounded by IdiotsThomas Erikson · 17 minIn Erikson's model, people act in four main styles, and you get through to anyone by meeting them on their terms, not yours.
  6. Games People PlayEric Berne · 18 minMuch of what people do together is a game: a set of moves with a hidden message that ends in a predictable payoff. Spot the game, and you can stop playing it.
  7. How to Talk to AnyoneLeil Lowndes · 16 minSuccess with people comes from small moves you can learn, and every one of them aims at the same thing: making the other person feel special and at ease.
  8. The 48 Laws of PowerRobert Greene · 19 minPower is a game that everyone plays, and Greene says you do better when you see how it works: keep a calm face, hide your aims, manage your image, learn what people want, stop in time, and stay hard to pin down.
  9. How to Win Friends and Influence People in the Digital AgeDale Carnegie & Associates · 20 minCarnegie's old rules still work online: you win friends and influence people by leaving each one a little better, and that starts with real interest in them.
  10. Read People Like a BookPatrick King · 19 minYou cannot see inside another person, so reading people means making careful guesses from many small clues, never trusting one sure sign.
  11. Talking to StrangersMalcolm Gladwell · 19 minWe misread strangers because we start by believing them, trust faces and manner that do not show feelings, and ignore the setting, so we should meet them with caution and humility.
  12. InfluenceRobert B. Cialdini · 19 minMost of our yes decisions come from a few mental shortcuts. Each shortcut usually points the right way, and each can be used on us by someone who wants a yes.
  13. You’re Not ListeningKate Murphy · 18 minPeople long to be heard, but most of us have stopped really listening. Good listening means being curious about what is in another person’s mind, and it is a skill you can build.
  14. Talk Like TEDCarmine Gallo · 18 minThe talks people love reach the heart first, then give a fresh idea in a way people can see, feel and remember, in the speaker's own voice.
  15. How to Make People Like You in 90 Seconds or LessNicholas Boothman · 18 minPeople like people who are like them, so you can build rapport fast: pick a warm attitude, greet openly, match their body and voice, ask open questions and listen.
  16. Power QuestionsAndrew Sobel, Jerold Panas · 18 minThe right question at the right moment can do more than any answer: it builds trust, wins business and moves people.
  17. Pre-SuasionRobert Cialdini · 19 minBefore you ask for a yes, steer what the other person is thinking about. That moment can matter more than the ask itself.
  18. Words That Change MindsShelle Rose Charvet · 18 minHow a person talks shows what moves them. Ask a few questions, hear the pattern, and use words that match it.
  19. Active Listening TechniquesNixaly Leonardo · 17 minPeople listen to you when they feel heard, so start by listening with your whole self.
  20. How to Talk So Kids Will Listen & Listen So Kids Will TalkAdele Faber, Elaine Mazlish · 18 minChildren listen, cooperate and grow when you accept their feelings and talk without blame, so their self-respect stays whole.
  21. Just ListenMark Goulston · 18 minTo get through to someone, get them talking. Say the feeling they are hiding, listen, and they start to say yes.
  22. Negotiation HacksSimon Rycraft · 17 minNegotiating well is six habits anyone can learn, built on preparation and trust, not on tricks.
  23. Never Split the DifferenceChris Voss, Tahl Raz · 18 minPeople decide with feelings, not logic, so listen until the other side feels understood, then guide them with questions.
  24. CuesVanessa Van Edwards · 18 minPeople judge you on warmth and competence, and they read both from tiny signals called cues. You can learn to read them and to send them on purpose.
  25. How to Get On with AnyoneCatherine Stothart · 17 minPeople deal with others in one of four styles, each driven by a different need, so read their style, adjust your own, and you can get on with anyone.
  26. How to Know a PersonDavid Brooks · 18 minThe skill under every good relationship is seeing another person deeply and making them feel seen. It can be learned.
  27. The 11 Laws of LikabilityMichelle Tillis Lederman · 18 minPeople do business with people they like, so build real connections instead of working a room: be yourself, be curious, give first and be patient.
  28. The Art of Reading MindsHenrik Fexeus · 19 minPeople show much of what they think and feel in their body, face and voice, often without knowing it. If you notice it and adapt to it, you understand people better and they follow you more easily.
  29. The Art of WarSun Tzu · 18 minThe best win costs the least: you prepare, you learn what the enemy will do, and you win before the fight starts, or without a fight at all.
