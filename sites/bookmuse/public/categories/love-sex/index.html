Love & Sex book summaries

18 books, most read first. Each one is a short course of visual lessons.

  1. BoundariesDr. Henry Cloud, Dr. John Townsend · 20 minA boundary is the property line of your life: it shows what you own and what is someone else’s, and setting it clearly lets you give a real yes and a real no instead of giving out of fear or guilt.
  2. Men Are from Mars, Women Are from VenusJohn Gray · 19 minMost clashes between a man and a woman come from forgetting they are different, and love lasts when each learns what the other needs.
  3. The Art of LovingErich Fromm · 17 minLove is not a feeling that happens to you but a skill of giving, learned through knowledge and daily practice, and it needs a person who has grown up enough to care.
  4. All About Lovebell hooks · 19 minLove is not a feeling that happens to us. It is a choice we make again and again, and we learn it by telling the truth, treating people fairly and facing our fear.
  5. Women Who Love Too MuchRobin Norwood · 19 minLoving too much means obsessing about a man who cannot love you back and calling it love. The way out is to turn all that effort toward yourself.
  6. Why Does He Do That?Lundy Bancroft · 25 minAbuse comes from a man's attitudes of ownership and entitlement, not from his feelings, so a partner cannot fix him by understanding him or by being easier to live with.
  7. The Art of SeductionRobert Greene · 20 minSeduction is a game of the mind, not of looks. A seducer builds a strong character, learns what the other person lacks, and leads them slowly into a spell.
  8. Drama FreeNedra Glover Tawwab · 18 minYou cannot change your family, but you can change how you take part in it, and that is enough to keep a hard relationship on your terms or to end it.
  9. How to Be an Adult in RelationshipsDavid Richo · 16 minLove is five things: attention, acceptance, appreciation, affection and allowing. Being an adult in love means giving them and asking for them, even when your past, your fears and your ego get in the way.
  10. Mindful Relationship HabitsBarrie Davenport, S.J. Scott · 17 minLove fades mostly from lack of attention, and a couple can bring it back by choosing small, mindful habits, one at a time.
  11. The 5 Love LanguagesGary Chapman · 16 minLove lasts when you learn which of five love languages your spouse understands best, and you choose to speak it.
  12. Hold Me TightSue Johnson · 19 minAdult love is a need for one safe person, so most fights are cries that ask are you there for me, and love lasts when partners learn to ask that clearly and answer with care.
  13. 8 Rules of LoveJay Shetty · 19 minLove is not a prize you find. It is a skill you practice every day: first with yourself, then with a partner, through hard times, and with everyone.
  14. Calling in “The One”Katherine Woodward Thomas · 18 minSearching harder for the right person will not change your love life. Becoming someone a great love can grow with will.
  15. How to Be AloneLane Moore · 17 minWhen a home was never safe, you can feel alone in a crowd. The way through is to care for yourself, let kind people in, and say what you need.
  16. Single On PurposeJohn Kim · 17 minYou cannot build a healthy bond with anyone until you build one with yourself, so use your single time, and your time inside a relationship, to know who you are.
  17. Love Unfu*kedGary John Bishop · 17 minA good relationship does not come from the right partner. You build it by managing yourself and keeping your word.
  18. Modern RomanceAziz Ansari, Eric Klinenberg · 18 minToday we search for a soul mate through screens and endless options, so finding love takes more care: treat people as people, not as bubbles on a screen, and give them real time.
