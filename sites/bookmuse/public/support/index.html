Questions, answered.

What is Bookmuse?

Bookmuse turns the big ideas of great books into short, visual lessons for iPhone and iPad. Each summary has an intro with the core idea, five to eight lessons of two or three minutes with diagrams, guess-first questions and small things to try, a quiz and the key takeaways.

Is it free?

Bookmuse is free to download. Every day one summary is free to read in full, the same one for everyone, until 00:00 UTC. Every other summary opens as a preview: the intro and the first three lessons. Pro opens every summary and gives 10 GB of cloud storage and more Bookmuse AI. It is sold monthly or yearly in the App Store, and the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel Pro or restore a purchase?

Open Bookmuse, then Settings → Subscription. Manage Subscription lets you change your plan or cancel in the App Store; Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for. Restore Purchases and Redeem Code are on the same page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the App Store first.

Do I need an account?

To read summaries, yes: a summary you open is kept in your account, so your place and progress follow you. You can also try a summary in your browser without one. Your own books, voices, saved words and review work without an account.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26, and Mac computers with Apple silicon. Your own books can also be managed from a computer at bookmuse-app.wanngo.com.

Which books are there?

Popular non-fiction across habits, money, psychology, work, relationships and more, with new books every week. Summaries are in English. Missing a book you'd like? Email us.

Can I make my own lessons with AI?

Yes. Add the Bookmuse MCP server to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Claude Code, sign in and allow access. Ask for a lesson on any topic and the agent adds it to your Library as an interactive page, diagrams and quizzes included. Agents manage your own books, words, highlights and folders; they can't see summaries. The steps are in Settings → Connections → AI Agents.

Can I read my own books too?

Yes. Import DRM-free EPUB, PDF, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), FB2, TXT and Markdown files, HTML notes and comics (CBZ, CBR) from Files, Google Drive, Dropbox or a catalog such as Project Gutenberg. Books from stores that add DRM can't be opened. Bookmuse Voices read them aloud for free on your device, and Bookmuse AI explains words and answers questions about the page.

My progress isn't syncing

Sign in to the same account on every device. Devices catch up when Bookmuse opens or when you pull down on Home. Settings → Account shows the sync status.

Is my reading private?

Yes. No ads, no tracking. With Bookmuse AI, what you look up and ask goes to our AI provider to be answered. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Bookmuse Settings → Account → Delete Account, or these steps if you can't open the app. Erase All Data in Settings clears your library and keeps the account.
