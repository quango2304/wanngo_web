Troubleshooting

My books or words aren't syncing

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Bookmuse account (Bookmuse Settings → Account), with the same Apple, Google or email sign-in. Devices catch up when Bookmuse opens or comes back to the screen, or when you pull down on Home. Settings → Account shows the sync status; if it says it couldn't sync, check your connection and tap Try Again. Large books can take a few minutes to arrive on a new device.

Translate asks to download a language

Apple Translation works offline once a language is downloaded. The first lookup in a new language pair asks to download it from Apple. If a download doesn't start, check your connection, or manage languages in the Settings app → Apps → Translate → Downloaded Languages.

I don't see word roots, meanings or Ask AI

Apple Translation gives the translation only. Roots, meanings, follow-ups and Ask AI come from BookMuse AI, which needs you to be signed in. Signed in and still seeing only a translation? BookMuse AI may be offline, or you may have reached its daily or monthly limit. Try again later.

Bookmuse doesn't appear as my Translate app

Open the Settings app → Apps → Default Apps → Translation and choose Bookmuse. This needs iOS or iPadOS 26.

A book won't open

Bookmuse opens DRM-free EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), FB2, TXT and Markdown books. Books bought from stores that add DRM can't be opened. If a DRM-free book still fails, email it to us (or a link to it) and we'll take a look.

How do I delete my data?

To delete your account and everything in it, go to Bookmuse Settings → Account → Delete Account, or follow these steps if you can't open the app. To keep the account but clear your library on all your devices, use Erase All Data in Bookmuse Settings. See the privacy policy for more.

Questions, answered.

How do I get Bookmuse?

Download it free from the App Store for iPhone and iPad. The Android app is coming soon to Google Play.

Is it free?

Yes. Bookmuse is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. A free account includes BookMuse AI with a daily allowance and 250 MB of cloud storage for your book files; books kept on your device have no limit.

Do I need an account?

No. Reading, Apple Translation, voices, saved words and review all work without one. Sign in with Apple, Google or email to turn on sync, BookMuse AI, the Feed, sharing, Google Drive and Dropbox, and AI agents.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26. Android 9 or later when the Android app arrives. The web dashboard at bookmuse-app.wanngo.com works in any modern browser.

Where do the books come from?

You bring them. Import DRM-free EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), FB2, TXT or Markdown books from Files, Google Drive or Dropbox, browse thousands of free classics from Project Gutenberg, or connect your own book server (Calibre, Komga, Stump, or any OPDS catalog). Bookmuse also has ready-made summaries you can read and listen to.

Is listening free?

Yes. Bookmuse Voices (English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese and Vietnamese) and Apple's voices run on your device, cost nothing and need no account.

Do I need an AI subscription or an API key?

No. Sign in and BookMuse AI gives rich word cards, follow-up questions and Ask AI with nothing to set up.

Prefer to keep lookups on your device? Stay signed out and lookups use Apple Translation, which is free and works offline once a language is downloaded.

Which languages can I learn?

Any language pair Apple Translation supports, and with BookMuse AI, almost any language it understands. Bookmuse itself speaks English, Vietnamese and Spanish.

How do AI agents use my library?

Bookmuse has an MCP server. Add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Claude Code, sign in, and allow access; the agent can then search your books, highlights and words, add words, organise folders and add books. The steps are in the app under Settings › Connections › AI Agents, and on the web dashboard. Agents can't delete your account or use BookMuse AI.

How do I make Bookmuse my default Translate app?

On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, then Apps → Default Apps → Translation, and choose Bookmuse. Now select text in any app, tap Translate, and Bookmuse shows the meaning with one tap to save it.

Is my reading private?

Yes. There are no ads and no tracking. Without an account, your books, words and progress stay on your device. Sign in and they are stored in your Bookmuse account so they sync to your other devices and the web; you can delete your account at any time. With BookMuse AI, what you look up and ask is sent to our AI provider to be answered; stay signed out to keep lookups on your device with Apple Translation. See the privacy policy for the details.
