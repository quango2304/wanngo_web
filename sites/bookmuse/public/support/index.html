Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads and no in-app purchases. A free account includes BookMuse AI with a daily allowance and 250 MB of cloud storage. Books kept on your device have no limit.

Do I need an account?

No. Reading, Apple Translation, voices, saved words and review all work without one. Sign in to turn on sync, BookMuse AI, the Feed, sharing, Google Drive and Dropbox, and AI agents.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26. Android 9 or later, in a closed test on Google Play for now: how to join. The web dashboard works in any modern browser.

Which books can I open?

DRM-free EPUB, PDF, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), FB2, TXT and Markdown, plus HTML notes, from Files, Google Drive, Dropbox, Project Gutenberg or your own Calibre, Komga, Stump or OPDS server. Books from stores that add DRM can't be opened. If a DRM-free book fails, email it to us.

Is listening free?

Yes. Bookmuse Voices (English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Vietnamese) and Apple's voices run on your device and need no account.

Do I need an AI subscription or API key?

No. Sign in and BookMuse AI gives word cards, follow-up questions and Ask AI. If you see only a translation, sign in, or BookMuse AI may be offline or at its daily or monthly limit.

Which languages can Bookmuse translate?

Any pair Apple Translation supports, and almost any language with BookMuse AI. Apple Translation works offline once a language is downloaded (Settings app → Apps → Translate → Downloaded Languages). Bookmuse itself speaks English, Vietnamese and Spanish.

My books or words aren't syncing

Sign in to the same account on every device. Devices catch up when Bookmuse opens or when you pull down on Home. Settings → Account shows the sync status. Large books can take a few minutes.

How do I make Bookmuse my Translate app?

On iOS or iPadOS 26, open the Settings app → Apps → Default Apps → Translation and choose Bookmuse.

How do AI agents use my library?

Add the Bookmuse MCP server to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Claude Code, sign in and allow access. Agents can search your books, highlights and words, add words, organise folders and add books. The steps are in Settings › Connections › AI Agents.

Is my reading private?

Yes. No ads, no tracking. Without an account, everything stays on your device. With BookMuse AI, what you look up and ask goes to our AI provider to be answered. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Bookmuse Settings → Account → Delete Account, or these steps if you can't open the app. Erase All Data in Settings clears your library and keeps the account.
