Questions, answered.

What is Bookmuse?

Bookmuse is a reading app for iPhone and iPad, a companion for people who love books. Import your own books, hear them read aloud, ask AI about what you're reading, open ready-made summaries, and keep the words you meet.

Is Bookmuse free?

Yes, with no ads and no in-app purchases. A free account includes BookMuse AI with a daily allowance and 250 MB of cloud storage. Books kept on your device have no limit.

Which books can I open?

DRM-free EPUB, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), FB2, TXT and Markdown, from Files, Google Drive, Dropbox, Project Gutenberg or your own Calibre, Komga, Stump or OPDS server. Books from stores that add DRM can't be opened.

Can it read my books aloud?

Yes, for free. Bookmuse Voices read English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese and Vietnamese right on your device, with the text following along. They need no account.

Do I need an account?

No. Reading, voices, saved words and review all work without one. Sign in to turn on sync, BookMuse AI, the Feed, sharing, Google Drive and Dropbox, and AI agents.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26. The Android app is coming to Google Play, for Android 9 or later. The web dashboard works in any modern browser.
