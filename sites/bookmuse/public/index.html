How a summary works

A whole book, one small lesson at a time.

Every summary is built the same way, so you always know where you are and how long is left.

  1. The core idea

    One sentence that holds the whole book, and the lessons ahead.

  2. Five to eight lessons

    One big idea each, two or three minutes, with diagrams that move and things to try.

  3. A quick quiz

    Questions that check you got it, and show you why when you didn't.

  4. Key takeaways

    What to keep and use, on one screen, for when you come back.

Android

Bookmuse on Android, in three steps.

The Android app is in a closed test on Google Play. It's free to join with the Google account you use on your phone. On an e-ink tablet such as a Boox, E-ink Mode keeps it still and black and white.

  1. Join the group

    Open the Bookmuse Testers group and tap Join group. Use the same Google account as Google Play on your phone.

  2. Open Bookmuse on Google Play

    On your Android phone, open the Bookmuse page on Google Play. It can take a few minutes to appear after you join.

  3. Install

    Tap Install like any app. Updates arrive from Google Play.

On a computer instead? Open the test page and tap Become a tester. Android 9 or later. Need help? Contact support.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Bookmuse?

Bookmuse turns the big ideas of great books into short, visual lessons for iPhone, iPad and Android. Each summary has an intro, five to eight lessons of two or three minutes with diagrams and small things to try, a quiz and the key takeaways.

Is it free?

Bookmuse is free to download. Every day one summary is free to read in full, the same one for everyone, and every other summary opens as a preview. Pro opens every summary and adds more cloud storage and AI.

How long does a summary take?

Each lesson takes two or three minutes, and a summary has five to eight of them, so you can learn a book in a few short sittings and pick up where you stopped.

Which books are there?

Popular non-fiction across habits, money, psychology, work, relationships and more, with new books added every week. Summaries are in English.

Is there an Android app?

Yes. The Android app is in a closed test on Google Play, free to join: three steps and you can install it. It runs on Android 9 or later, on phones, tablets and e-ink readers such as Boox.

Can I make my own lessons?

Yes. Connect an AI agent such as Claude or ChatGPT to your Bookmuse library and ask it for a lesson on any topic. It adds an interactive lesson to your Library, diagrams and quizzes included.

Can I read my own books too?

Yes. Import EPUB, PDF, Kindle and other DRM-free files, hear them read aloud by voices that run on your device, ask AI about the page and keep the words you look up.
